Launch date

  • ANNOUNCED November 4, 2026, 3:00 p.m., global launch. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Source inconsistency: “Carve a New Path” says launches November 4 at 3:00 p.m. PDT globally and later November 4, 2026, at 3:00 p.m. PST; the What’s Next recap says 3:00 p.m. PST. The French articles say le 5 novembre 2026 à minuit (heure française). This wiki does not assert a time zone. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Access

  • VERIFIED access will be included with your World of Warcraft Subscription or Game Time. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Product page: Included with an Active WoW Subscription; Available on Windows/Mac. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]

Editions

  • VERIFIED Optional upgrade editions: Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack and the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED The Skyborne Epic Pack includes everything in the Skyborne Heroic Pack, plus beta access beginning September 17, 30 days of World of Warcraft Game Time starting November 4, additional cosmetics, pets, tabards, and three total Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]
