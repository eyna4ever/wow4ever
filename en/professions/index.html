Professions

  • VERIFIED Professions are also gaining more depth, with over 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges, adjusted crafted food that can grant a small experience bonus and stat boosts, and advanced Blueprint recipes from dungeon bosses. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): A number of recipes intended for Blacksmithing and Leatherworking as random drops in dungeons are now available. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Mage only: Comprehension: This new Mage-specific minor tradeskill allow Mages to read and decipher scrolls dropped by creatures in the world. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Mage and Shaman, 2026-10-07]
  • NOT YET DETAILED The full recipe list is not published in the saved sources. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Camping

  • VERIFIED The system expands the simple Cooking Fire from the Cooking profession into a Basic Campfire. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Placing a campfire creates a campsite where players can rest and gain access to useful benefits such as vendors, repairs, profession workspaces, and one-hour buffs. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED A Basic Campfire allows up to three special crafted objects to be added by nearby players. Every profession has three different objects learned at different skill levels. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED The first campsite objects are learned once a profession reaches skill level 20, either from special nonplayer characters (NPCs) who introduce players to camping or from profession trainers, while more advanced objects come from Blueprint recipes that drop from specific dungeon bosses. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Alchemists can learn to place an Alchemy Lab, Leatherworkers can place a Tanning Rack required for more advanced Leatherworking recipes, and Cooks can learn upgraded Campfires that allow five or 10 objects to be placed at a camp. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): Campfires no longer deal damage to players below level 5, and they deal reduced damage to players with less than 90% health. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Sep 24): Players may now obtain a Campfire buff if they are sitting, sitting in a chair, sleeping, lying down, or using the kneeling emote. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]

Legacy system

  • VERIFIED Players earn Legacy Points by completing Legacy Challenges across several categories, with examples including leveling a specific class or reaching a particular skill milestone. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Legacy Points can be spent in Legacy Trees, beginning with Professions, Adventure, and Resourcefulness. Example talent: “Bountiful Harvest”. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Legacy Points are shared across all alts, while each character spends those points separately in their own trees. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • ANNOUNCED At launch, each character can spend up to 16 points in their Legacy Trees. earn up to 65 Legacy Points at the start. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Reward track: special cosmetic or prestige rewards, such as the Spectral Bear Cub, without providing a competitive gameplay advantage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Collections and Legacy Points remain account-wide across rulesets, with Hardcore as the exception. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Complete challenges to earn Legacy Points that unlock account-wide perks like faster rested XP and reduced campfire cooldowns [@Warcraft — Legacy System (Sep 13), 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED PvP: three seasonal tasks per season grant a Legacy Point. [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works, 2026-10-07]
