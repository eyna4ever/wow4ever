Is World of Warcraft: Forever a new version of Classic?

  • VERIFIED No: This is not a mode, a season, or a new version of Classic. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

When does it launch?

  • ANNOUNCED November 4, 2026, 3:00 p.m. (the English sources give both PDT and PST; see Launch & access). [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Do I need to buy it?

  • VERIFIED access will be included with your World of Warcraft Subscription or Game Time. Editions are optional. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]

On which platforms?

  • VERIFIED Product page: Available on Windows/Mac. No other platform is stated. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]

What is the maximum level?

  • VERIFIED Players adventure to level 60. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]

Are there flying mounts or level scaling?

  • VERIFIED no flying mounts, no level scaling. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

How do realms work?

  • VERIFIED World of Warcraft: Forever is going realmless; you pick a ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplaying). [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Can I group with friends on a different ruleset?

  • VERIFIED No: cross-ruleset grouping is not supported. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Is there a new race?

  • VERIFIED Yes, the Skyborne, who join the Horde or the Alliance at character creation. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Can I play with a controller?

  • VERIFIED Yes: official gamepad support. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Is Hardcore available at launch?

  • ANNOUNCED Hardcore is listed among content coming after the December 9 raids; no date given. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Which class should I pick first?

  • TO BE CONFIRMED Use the class comparison tool; its ratings are an editorial evaluation, not official data. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
