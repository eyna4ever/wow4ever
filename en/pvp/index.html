PvP

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

Progression

  • PvP progression has two parts: Honor and Rank Points. [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works]
  • Ranks have a weekly cap; ultimate titles High Warlord for the Horde or Grand Marshal for the Alliance. [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works]
  • Honor buys a blue-quality and an epic-quality set; the epic set requires the blue piece plus an Emboldened Seal (rank-gated). [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works]
  • Seasonal weekly tasks grant Honor and Rank Points; three per season grant a Legacy Point. [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works]

Darkspear Islands

  • 15v15 battleground off the coast of Dustwallow Marsh: Darkspear Raiders vs Theramore Expeditionary Force. [Battle for the Darkspear Islands in World of Warcraft: Forever] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • Four capture points plus a central flag; The first team to earn 2000 points wins! [Battle for the Darkspear Islands in World of Warcraft: Forever]
