Presentation

  • VERIFIED the chaotic City of Dalaran; investigating strange magical trouble in Dalaran. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED One of the nine new dungeons announced for WoW: Forever. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Level range & access

  • VERIFIED Levels 28–33 (official forum list). [Krol’dok Stronghold (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-15]
  • VERIFIED Beta notes (Oct 8): Now available for testing: the new City of Dalaran dungeon. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED The WoW: Forever Beta enters Week 4! Team up & take on the City of Dalaran [@Warcraft — Beta Week 4, 2026-10-08]
  • NOT YET DETAILED Entrance location and attunement/key requirements are not stated in the saved sources. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Bosses & mechanics

  • NOT YET DETAILED No boss, encounter or mechanic of this dungeon is named in the saved official sources (the dungeon design podcast of Oct 8/12 is a video not transcribed here). [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Observed in beta (players)

  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Bosses met in beta runs: Seven on a normal run: Atrexis the Grave Knight, Arcane Anomaly, Mana Devourer, Fel Ancient, Unstable Sentinel, the Arcanic Enigma (Mana Elemental) and the Shade of the Archmage. The Tome of Dalaran summons an eighth, Lyn the Ignored. WOWF.IO: There are eight bosses, one of them a rare. [MmonsteR (site) — beta report (community)] [WOWF.IO (fan site) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Contradiction explained: a fan database post says City of Dalaran, level 28, 9 bosses (count from game files); players report 7 + 1 summoned boss. The ninth entry has not been seen in play. [@wowforevergg (fan database site) — beta report (community)] [MmonsteR (site) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Route: Runs start with Atrexis the Grave Knight in the sewers under the mage city in the Alterac Mountains, then six more bosses wait in the streets in any order. (single report) [MmonsteR (site) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Shade of the Archmage (final boss): When his mana runs out ... polymorphs the whole group for 8 seconds, then channels Evocation for 6 seconds to refill it. (single report) [WOWF.IO (fan site) — beta report (community)]

Loot notes (all dungeons)

  • VERIFIED Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality and set bonuses have been improved to feel more useful and exciting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED If a bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare item drops in a dungeon, its appearance will be granted to all eligible looters when the item is looted. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Advanced Blueprint recipes that drop from specific dungeon bosses (which bosses is not stated). [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Related quests & experience

  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): Dungeon quests now reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 8): The experience earned from killing creatures in dungeons has been increased by approximately 20%. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • NOT YET DETAILED No other quest of this dungeon is named in the saved official sources. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
