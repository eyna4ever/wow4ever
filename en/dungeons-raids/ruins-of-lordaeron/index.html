Presentation

  • VERIFIED helping the Forsaken reclaim the Ruins of Lordaeron. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED One of the nine new dungeons announced for WoW: Forever. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Level range & access

  • VERIFIED Levels 15–20 (official forum list). [Krol’dok Stronghold (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-15]
  • VERIFIED Beta week 1: New Dungeon: Ruins of Lordaeron (Level 15–20). [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live, 2026-09-17]
  • NOT YET DETAILED Entrance location and attunement/key requirements are not stated in the saved sources. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Bosses & mechanics

  • NOT YET DETAILED No boss, encounter or mechanic of this dungeon is named in the saved official sources (the dungeon design podcast of Oct 8/12 is a video not transcribed here). [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Observed in beta (players)

  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Six bosses, in any order: There are 6 bosses in the Ruins of Lordaeron: Witherfang, The Baron, Viktor the Vile, The Abandoned, Bjork, and Rath’mael. Wowhead: Ruins of Lordaeron has a total of six bosses and can be done in any order: [Method (site) — beta report (community)] [Jurdi (Wowhead news) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Contradiction explained: a fan database post counts Ruins of Lordaeron, level 15, 7 bosses; a fan wiki describes six regular bosses, two of which must be summoned, plus a rare spawn. Players meet six bosses; the seventh is most likely the rare spawn. [@wowforevergg (fan database site) — beta report (community)] [Warcraft Forever Wiki (fan wiki) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Summoned bosses: Viktor only appears after players trigger an event by clicking on the chimney located in the house to the southwest of Lordamere Overlook. You fight 5 waves of adds before the boss. The Abandoned: After 3 waves of adds, The Abandoned comes out on wave 4. [Method (site) — beta report (community)] [Jurdi (Wowhead news) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Known beta bug: On the Beta, Viktor the Vile appears to have a bug where he can potentially not spawn. (single report) [Jurdi (Wowhead news) — beta report (community)]
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Level range: the official range stays 15–20. Player guides suggest a little higher: non-linear dungeon for players level 16-22. / a level 16-23 Dungeon. [Method (site) — beta report (community)] [Jurdi (Wowhead news) — beta report (community)]

Loot notes (all dungeons)

  • VERIFIED Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality and set bonuses have been improved to feel more useful and exciting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED If a bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare item drops in a dungeon, its appearance will be granted to all eligible looters when the item is looted. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Advanced Blueprint recipes that drop from specific dungeon bosses (which bosses is not stated). [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Related quests & experience

  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): Dungeon quests now reward 50% less extra experience beyond normal quest values. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 8): The experience earned from killing creatures in dungeons has been increased by approximately 20%. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • NOT YET DETAILED No other quest of this dungeon is named in the saved official sources. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
