Official dungeon level ranges

  • VERIFIED Kaivax's official list (Sep 15): The Hall of Thanes 13–18; Ruins of Lordaeron 15–20; Excavation Site: Wetlands 24–29; City of Dalaran 28–33; The Drowned City 35–40; Krol’dok Stronghold 40–45; Alcaz Prison 48–53; Blackmaw Hold 55–60; Shaper’s Terrace 58–60. [Krol’dok Stronghold (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-15]
  • VERIFIED Update: New Dungeon - Excavation Site: Wetlands (26-31). (Oct 1 beta notes; replaces 24–29). [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1) also lists existing dungeons Ragefire Chasm, Wailing Caverns, Razorfen Downs (25+) and Uldaman (30+) as available. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]

Dungeons

  • VERIFIED nine new dungeons: the Hall of Thanes beneath Ironforge, the Ruins of Lordaeron, a mysterious excavation site above Whelgar’s Excavation, the chaotic City of Dalaran, Blackmaw Hold, the Drowned City, Krol’dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, and the Shaper’s Terrace (quoted as written; one is not formally named). [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Unique dungeon boss items are now blue quality; hundreds of new drops have been added. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Raids

  • VERIFIED The Barrow Deeps is planned as a maximum-level 10-player challenge, while Hyjal Summit will bring 20-player raid encounters. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • ANNOUNCED New raids unlock on December 9, followed by more raids, dungeons, PvP updates, Hardcore, a revamped iconic raid. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
