World of Warcraft: Forever — confirmed facts

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

Overview

  • Blizzard describes World of Warcraft: Forever as a full new World of Warcraft game set in a reimagined original Azeroth. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap]
  • Launch: November 4, 2026, 3:00 p.m. (the English article gives both PDT and PST; see Launch & access). [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • This is not a mode, a season, or a new version of Classic. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • Beta began September 17 and is available through October 21, the last full day of testing. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever] [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]
