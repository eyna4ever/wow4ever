Skyborne & class-race combos

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

Skyborne

  • New playable race from Zephras Isle; faction chosen at character creation. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • Horde-aligned Skyborne embrace elemental traditions and gain access to the Shaman class, while Alliance-aligned Skyborne pursue the arcane legacy of their ancestors and gain access to the Mage class. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • Both factions can also play Warriors, Hunters, Rogues, and Druids, with new Skyborne druid forms. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]

New class-race combos

  • Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, and Undead Paladin. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap]
  • Wording difference: the product page says the Forsaken Paladin or Dwarf Shaman; the Deep Dive says Undead Paladin. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)] [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap]
