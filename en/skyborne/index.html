Skyborne race page · All races

Skyborne

  • VERIFIED New playable race from Zephras Isle; faction chosen at character creation. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Horde-aligned Skyborne embrace elemental traditions and gain access to the Shaman class, while Alliance-aligned Skyborne pursue the arcane legacy of their ancestors and gain access to the Mage class. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Both factions can also play Warriors, Hunters, Rogues, and Druids, with new Skyborne druid forms. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Faction alignment — exactly what sources say

  • VERIFIED At character creation, you’ll choose whether your Skyborne joins the Horde or the Alliance. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Product page: Follow the path of the Windshaper Horde or the High Order Alliance in your quest to defend Zephras Isle. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]
  • VERIFIED Class access by faction: Horde-aligned Skyborne get Shaman, Alliance-aligned Skyborne get Mage; both factions get Warriors, Hunters, Rogues and Druids (see quote below). [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • TO BE CONFIRMED Not stated in the sources: whether the faction can be changed later, and the racial abilities in detail. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

New class-race combos

  • VERIFIED Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, and Undead Paladin. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Wording difference: the product page says the Forsaken Paladin or Dwarf Shaman; the Deep Dive says Undead Paladin. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)] [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
