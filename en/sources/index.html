Status legend
  • VERIFIED Verified: confirmed by an official Blizzard source, with its date shown.
  • ANNOUNCED Announced: officially planned, but not in the game yet.
  • TO BE CONFIRMED To be confirmed: plausible or from original WoW, not yet verified for Forever.
  • OBSOLETE Obsolete: replaced by newer official information.
  • NOT YET DETAILED Not yet detailed: Blizzard hasn't published this information yet.
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Community contribution: from a fan site, needs validation; never official.
