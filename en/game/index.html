The big picture: how Blizzard positions Forever, its setting and its design principles.

Positioning

  • VERIFIED Built as a new permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED This is not a mode, a season, or a new version of Classic. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Setting

  • VERIFIED Set after the events of Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, players will return to the original continents, adventure to level 60. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]

Design principles

  • VERIFIED Four pillars: approachable and familiar, the world as the main character, the journey before the destination, and protecting and nurturing the social experiences. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED no flying mounts, no level scaling. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
