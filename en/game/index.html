What World of Warcraft: Forever is

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

Positioning

  • Built as a new permanent home alongside modern and Classic WoW. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever]
  • This is not a mode, a season, or a new version of Classic. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]

Setting

  • Set after the events of Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, players will return to the original continents, adventure to level 60. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever]

Design principles

  • Four pillars: approachable and familiar, the world as the main character, the journey before the destination, and protecting and nurturing the social experiences. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
  • no flying mounts, no level scaling. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap]
