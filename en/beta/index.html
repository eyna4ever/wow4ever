Beta

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

Dates & access

  • Beta began September 17; access via opt-in selection or eligible bundles (Skyborne Epic Pack, Warcraft Forever Collection). [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever] [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]
  • The beta will be available through October 21, the last full day of testing. [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]

Content

  • the level cap will be set to 20 and will be raised to 30 later in the beta. [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]
  • Week 1: new race and zone Skyborne and Zephras Isle; Hall of Thanes (Level 13–18); Ruins of Lordaeron (Level 15–20); all zone content up to level 20. [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]
  • A “Server Slam” open window is announced; no date is given in the source. [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live]
