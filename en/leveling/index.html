  • TO BE CONFIRMED To be confirmed: plausible or from original WoW, not yet verified for Forever.

Levels 1–12

  • VERIFIED Zephras Isle: an elemental skybound starting experience from levels 1 through 12 (Skyborne). [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Paladin: Paladins gain Holy Strike at level 6. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Levels 10–20

  • VERIFIED Talents from level 10 (milestones at 11, 21, 31 and the new 16-point one). [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Rogue and Warlock, 2026-10-07] [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Hall of Thanes (Level 13–18); Ruins of Lordaeron (Level 15–20). [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live, 2026-09-17]
  • VERIFIED Several class abilities arrive at level 20, e.g. Consecration is also baseline at level 20; Aimed Shot is now granted as a baseline ability to all Hunters at level 20. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13] [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Levels 30–45

  • VERIFIED Riverglades: more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Level 40

  • VERIFIED Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Rogue and Warlock, 2026-10-07]
  • VERIFIED Mage: Frostfire Bolt: New spell learned at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Mage and Shaman, 2026-10-07]

Level 60

  • VERIFIED Level cap: adventure to level 60. [Carve a New Path with World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-12]
  • ANNOUNCED The Barrow Deeps is planned as a maximum-level 10-player challenge, while Hyjal Summit will bring 20-player raid encounters. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Not yet published

  • NOT YET DETAILED Level ranges for Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas and the other new dungeons are not given in the saved sources. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
