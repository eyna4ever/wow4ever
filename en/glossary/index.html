Skyborne

  • VERIFIED New playable race from Zephras Isle that joins the Horde or the Alliance. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Ruleset

  • VERIFIED Replaces realms: Normal, PvP or Roleplaying. Each ruleset functions more like its own large ecosystem than a single traditional realm. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Legacy

  • VERIFIED WoW: Forever’s Legacy system rewards account-wide benefits every time you progress a character’s adventure. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)] [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Camping

  • VERIFIED System that lets you craft campfires in the open world; first campsite objects at profession skill 20. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12] [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Honor

  • VERIFIED PvP currency used to buy PvP gear. [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works, 2026-10-07]

Rank Points

  • VERIFIED Earned from PvP activities; they advance your PvP rank (weekly cap). [How PvP Progression in World of Warcraft: Forever Works, 2026-10-07]

Milestone talent

  • VERIFIED One-point talents at 11, 21 and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Rogue and Warlock, 2026-10-07]

Dual Specialization

  • VERIFIED Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Rogue and Warlock, 2026-10-07]

Comprehension

  • VERIFIED This new Mage-specific minor tradeskill allow Mages to read and decipher scrolls dropped by creatures in the world. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Mage and Shaman, 2026-10-07]

Server Slam

  • VERIFIED A window during the beta when servers open to anyone; no date published. [The World of Warcraft: Forever Beta Now Live, 2026-09-17]

Hardcore

  • ANNOUNCED Listed among announced future content; no date. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Tank / healer / damage dealer

  • TO BE CONFIRMED Group roles. Blizzard's goal for Paladins: effective as tanks, healers, or Retribution melee damage dealers. General definitions come from original WoW. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
