Zones

  • VERIFIED Mount Hyjal: explore the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat and Darkwhisper Gorge. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Shen'dralas: a long-hidden mystery between Mulgore and Desolace. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Riverglades: more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Zephras Isle: an elemental skybound starting experience from levels 1 through 12. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

How the world works

  • VERIFIED You’ll feel that philosophy throughout the world: no flying mounts, no level scaling, and a continued focus on challenge, preparation, communication, and cooperation. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Your adventure begins across the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor with new and reimagined regions, unfinished corners of the original world, and more than a thousand new quests woven throughout the level 1–60 journey. You’ll help restore Hyjal, uncover forgotten history in Shen'dralas, build a future on the Riverglades frontier, and take your first steps through Zephras Isle. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

New regions in detail

  • VERIFIED Mount Hyjal invites you to explore the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat, journey through Darkwhisper Gorge, and help shape the future of a recovering land. In Shen'dralas, a long-hidden mystery between Mulgore and Desolace becomes a new adventure tied to the Shen’dralar, Eldre’Thalas, Dire Maul, and the centaur tribes of the region. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED In the Riverglades, you’ll discover a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities, and old grudges, with more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
