Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Undead racial abilities are being reshaped with the same design goals. Will of the Forsaken still removes Charm, Fear, and Sleep, but no longer grants immunity to those effects. Cannibalize now restores Mana as well as Health, increasing its usefulness for mana-using classes such as Mages, Warlocks, Paladins, and Priests. Underwater Breathing remains unchanged but continues to matter in old and new underwater combat situations, while Touch of the Grave gives Undead characters a new damage-focused racial with a survivability edge by giving attacks a chance to drain life from enemies. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Sep 24): Touch of the Grave no longer breaks CC or activates from spells and abilities that don’t have a damage component to them. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): Cannibalize cannot be used if the caster is immune to Physical damage. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 8): Fixed an issue where Will of the Forsaken could sometimes not be used while asleep or charmed. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]

Racial Priest spells

  • VERIFIED Priest Racial Abilities: Each race of Priest has two spells unique to their Race. They have been fully re-evaluated, and new ones have been added for Gnome Priests: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Touch of Weakness: Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Dark Sacrifice: Learned at level 20. Cannibalize your own health to gain Mana over time. Mana gain increased by Spirit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
