Lore, faction & starting zone

  • VERIFIED Product page: Stand with a new race of windswept elves as their once-secluded home faces an existential threat. Follow the path of the Windshaper Horde or the High Order Alliance in your quest to defend Zephras Isle. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]
  • VERIFIED At character creation, you’ll choose whether your Skyborne joins the Horde or the Alliance. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Starting zone: Zephras Isle, an elemental skybound starting experience from levels 1 through 12. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Beta quests: The High Order Skyborne quest “Catching Wind” has been redesigned; The Skyborne quest “Exploring the Horde” now completes the objective to talk with Lady Sylvanas Windrunner. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): High Order Skyborne can now do the quest “Power Over Poison” to learn the Cure Poison spell. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • NOT YET DETAILED Not stated in the sources: whether the faction can be changed later. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]

Overview

  • VERIFIED Faction: Horde and Alliance (chosen at character creation). Listed in the official Horde race and class table and Alliance race and class table. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) — Official description: The Windshapers are the descendants of the first shen’dorei high elves to receive the wind spirits’ gift of elemental skysight. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) — Official description: The High Order is made up of the descendants of the ancient Highborne scholars of Eldre’Thalas. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) — Walk on Air (active) : Glide downward through the air for 10 seconds [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) — Skysight (active) : Receive an Elemental Blessing increasing run speed by 10% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) — Wind Blessed (passive) : 1% increased melee, ranged, and spellcasting Haste [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) — Elemental Insight (passive) : Damage to Elementals increased by 5% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) — Walk on Air (active) : Glide downward through the air for 10 seconds [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) — Read Ley Line (active) : Activate a ley line to gain 100% increased Health and Mana regeneration [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) — Wind Blessed (passive) : 1% increased melee, ranged, and spellcasting Haste [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) — Elemental Insight (passive) : Damage to Elementals increased by 5% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Classes

  • VERIFIED Horde-aligned Skyborne embrace elemental traditions and gain access to the Shaman class, while Alliance-aligned Skyborne pursue the arcane legacy of their ancestors and gain access to the Mage class. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Both factions can also play Warriors, Hunters, Rogues, and Druids, with new Skyborne druid forms. [World of Warcraft: Forever What’s Next Panel Recap, 2026-09-12]
  • VERIFIED Playable classes (official race × class table): Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman (Horde), Mage (Alliance), Druid. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Not available per that table: Paladin, Priest, Warlock. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
