Racial abilities at a glance (official)

  • VERIFIED Human : Will to Survive, Perception, Sword Specialization (passive), The Human Spirit (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Dwarf : Stoneform, Find Treasure, Mace Specialization (passive), Big Game Hunter (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Night Elf : Elune’s Light, Shadowmeld, Quickness (passive), Wisp Spirit (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Gnome : Escape Artist, Eureka!, Expansive Mind (passive), Engineering Specialization (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED High Order Skyborne (Alliance) : Walk on Air, Read Ley Line, Wind Blessed (passive), Elemental Insight (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Orc : Blood Fury, Shatter Curse, Axe Specialization (passive), Hardiness (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Undead / Forsaken : Will of the Forsaken, Cannibalize, Underwater Breathing (passive), Touch of the Grave (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Tauren : War Stomp, Cultivation, Plainsrunning (passive), Endurance (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Troll : Berserking, Rapid Regeneration, Beast Slaying (passive), Regeneration (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Windshaper Skyborne (Horde) : Walk on Air, Skysight, Wind Blessed (passive), Elemental Insight (passive). [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED The team is also adding six new class-and-race combinations to explore: Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, and Undead Paladin, with more planned for future updates. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Druid deep dive: Racial abilities: Now usable while shapeshifted. This includes Shadowmeld, War Stomp, and Walk on Air. (the source does not say which race each belongs to) [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • TO BE CONFIRMED This wiki lists only Forever-specific official information; original-WoW racial data is deliberately not shown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
