Overview

  • VERIFIED Official description: Always the tauren strive to preserve the balance of nature and heed the will of their goddess, the Earth Mother. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED War Stomp (active) : Stuns nearby enemies for 2 seconds [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Cultivation (active) : Grow bonus herbs that don’t require herbalism to gather [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Plainsrunning (passive) : Gain increased movement speed the longer you stay moving [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Endurance (passive) : Total Health increased by 5% and Hit Chance increased by 1% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Sep 24): Cultivation now has a player-level requirement for every Herb. The level required is equal to the Herb’s level required to pick it divided by 5. Herbs that require a higher than 300 Herbalism skill to pick can be Cultivated at player level 60. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]

Classes

  • VERIFIED Playable classes (official race × class table): Warrior, Hunter, Shaman, Druid. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Not available per that table: Paladin, Rogue, Priest, Mage, Warlock. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Not yet detailed

  • NOT YET DETAILED The starting zone for this race in Forever is not stated in the saved official sources. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Source discrepancy

  • VERIFIED The English article gives Endurance as Total Health increased by 5% and Hit Chance increased by 1%.; the French version mentions only the 5% health increase. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
