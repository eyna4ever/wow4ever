Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Sep 24): Changed Eureka on every class to a 10% discount on Mana, Rage, or Energy abilities. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 1): Eureka! no longer benefits periodic effects at all. Channeled spells do not count as periodics. [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 8 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]

Racial Priest spells

  • VERIFIED Priest Racial Abilities: Each race of Priest has two spells unique to their Race. They have been fully re-evaluated, and new ones have been added for Gnome Priests: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Confounding Flash: Learned at level 10. Confuses up to 5 nearby enemies for 3 seconds. Effect broken by taking damage. 0.5 second cast time. 2-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Contingency Plan: Learned at level 20. Places a ward on a friendly target for 30 seconds. If that target’s health drops below 35%, the target is instantly protected by a damage absorb shield and gains a heal-over-time effect. These two effects can be stacked with Renew and Power Word: Shield. 5-minute cooldown. Instant cast. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Classes
