Overview

  • VERIFIED Faction: Alliance. Listed in the official Alliance race and class table. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Official description: Ten thousand years ago, the night elves founded a vast empire, but their reckless use of primal magic brought them to ruin. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Elune’s Light (active) : Increases critical chance by 10% for 15 seconds [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Shadowmeld (active) : Gain Stealth while immobile [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Quickness (passive) : 1% increased Dodge chance and 2% increased run speed [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Wisp Spirit (passive) : 75% increased run speed while dead [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial Priest spells

  • VERIFIED Priest Racial Abilities: Each race of Priest has two spells unique to their Race. They have been fully re-evaluated, and new ones have been added for Gnome Priests: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Starshards: Still learned at level 10. Damage increased substantially but now has a 30 second cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Elune’s Grace: Still learned at level 20. Now reduces the chance for melee and ranged attacks to hit the Priest by 50% for 15 seconds or until 3 attacks have missed. 5-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Classes

  • VERIFIED Playable classes (official race × class table): Warrior, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Druid. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Not available per that table: Paladin, Shaman, Mage, Warlock. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Not yet detailed

  • NOT YET DETAILED The starting zone for this race in Forever is not stated in the saved official sources. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
