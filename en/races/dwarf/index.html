Overview

  • VERIFIED Official description: In ages past the dwarves cared only for riches taken from the earth’s depths. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]

Racial abilities in Forever

  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft: Forever, racial abilities have been reworked to feel stronger, more distinct, and more meaningful without pushing every race toward the same strengths. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Each race now has two active abilities and two passive abilities, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Stoneform (active) : Immunity to Bleeds, Poisons, and Diseases and reduce Physical damage taken for 8 seconds [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Find Treasure (active) : Track nearby treasure chests [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Mace Specialization (passive) : Maces increase spell and ability critical chance by 1% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Big Game Hunter (passive) : Damage to Beasts increased by 5% [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Dwarves, for example, keep Stoneform’s focus on removing and granting immunity to Bleeds, Poisons, and Diseases, but its defensive bonus now reduces Physical damage taken instead of increasing Armor, making it useful to more classes. Find Treasure can now remain active alongside other tracking abilities, Mace Specialization increases critical strike chance with all spells and abilities while a mace is equipped, and Big Game Hunter grants increased damage against Beasts, giving Dwarf characters moments where they can shine in the right encounters. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Racial Priest spells

  • VERIFIED Priest Racial Abilities: Each race of Priest has two spells unique to their Race. They have been fully re-evaluated, and new ones have been added for Gnome Priests: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Desperate Prayer: Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Chastise: Learned at level 20. Damages and immobilizes a target for 2 seconds. Only useable on Humanoids. Instant cast, 2-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Classes

  • VERIFIED New class-race combination: Dwarf Shaman. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Product page: Dwarf Shaman. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]
  • VERIFIED Playable classes (official race × class table): Warrior, Paladin, Hunter, Rogue, Priest, Shaman. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Not available per that table: Mage, Warlock, Druid. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
