A step-by-step reading path through this wiki for new players. Follow the steps in order; each one links to the page to read and tells you what you'll learn there.

Status legend
  • VERIFIED Verified: confirmed by an official Blizzard source, with its date shown.
  • ANNOUNCED Announced: officially planned, but not in the game yet.
  • TO BE CONFIRMED To be confirmed: not yet confirmed by an official World of Warcraft: Forever source.
  • OBSOLETE Obsolete: replaced by newer official information.
  • NOT YET DETAILED Not yet detailed: Blizzard hasn't published this information yet.
  • COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION Community contribution: from a fan site, needs validation; never official.
  • EDITORIAL EVALUATION Editorial evaluation: the wiki editors' opinion, not official data.

Steps

  1. Read the beginner FAQ — you'll find the essentials: what Forever is, access, platforms, max level.
  2. Understand the game — you'll find how Blizzard positions Forever and its design principles.
  3. Check launch & access — you'll find the release date and what you need to play.
  4. Pick a race — you'll find the playable races and their racial abilities.
  5. Pick a class — you'll find the nine classes, then the comparison tool to narrow your choice.
  6. Learn the systems — you'll find rulesets instead of realms, visual options, controllers.
  7. Plan your leveling — you'll find zones and dungeons by level range from 1 to 60.
  8. Choose professions — you'll find professions, Camping and the Legacy system.
  9. Prepare for dungeons — you'll find the new dungeons and announced raids.
  10. Keep the glossary handy — you'll find Forever-specific terms explained.
  11. Follow the news — you'll find what's new and what's announced.

How to read the badges

Every fact carries a badge: VERIFIED (official source), ANNOUNCED (planned, not live yet), NOT YET DETAILED (Blizzard hasn't covered it yet), TO BE CONFIRMED (not verified for Forever), OBSOLETE (replaced by newer info), COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTION (fan source, needs validation). Open “Status legend” at the top of each page for details.
