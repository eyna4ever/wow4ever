General

  • VERIFIED revamps to all classes and specializations. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]
  • VERIFIED Talents from level 10 with milestone talents at 11, 21 and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone; Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30] [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Abilities may continue to evolve throughout the beta test. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Hunter

  • VERIFIED Aimed Shot is now granted as a baseline ability to all Hunters at level 20. Traps usable in combat (30-second cooldown); Foxes are a new pet family; Pets now get increased stats based on their Hunter’s gear. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Druid

  • VERIFIED Shapeshifted Druids can use more consumables, benefit from weapon effects, interact with more objects, and use racial abilities without leaving form. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Priest

  • VERIFIED Fear Ward and Devouring Plague are now available to all Priests at level 20. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Warrior

  • VERIFIED Adjustments that make Rage more reliable. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
