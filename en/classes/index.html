Classes

Status: Confirmed Every fact below comes from a cited official Blizzard source.

General

  • revamps to all classes and specializations. [World of Warcraft: Forever (product page)]
  • Talents from level 10 with milestone talents at 11, 21 and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone; Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid] [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior]
  • Abilities may continue to evolve throughout the beta test. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid]

Hunter & Druid

  • Aimed Shot is now granted as a baseline ability to all Hunters at level 20. Traps usable in combat (30-second cooldown); Foxes are a new pet family; Pets now get increased stats based on their Hunter’s gear. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid]

Priest & Warrior

  • Fear Ward and Devouring Plague are now available to all Priests at level 20. Warriors: adjustments that make Rage more reliable. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior]
