1. Understand

Official overview (quotes)

  • VERIFIED Walking the Path of Light and Shadow with the Priest Class [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Priests can shape their path through Discipline, Holy, and Shadow, offering support through protection, healing, and damage. In World of Warcraft: Forever, the Priest continues to draw from the power of Light and Shadow, with updates that expand its toolkit while keeping the class’s familiar role as a versatile spellcaster intact. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Their updates focus on expanding flexibility across Discipline, Holy, and Shadow while preserving the class’s command of healing, protection, Light, and Shadow. Fear Ward and Devouring Plague are now available to all Priests at level 20, Divine Spirit is baseline at level 30, and Shadow Word: Death joins the class as a baseline ability at level 32, while refreshed racial abilities and talent updates add new options for support, healing, and damage-focused play. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED As Shadow, Priests can harness the power of darkness to deal powerful damage-over-time (DoT) but lack the full utility in that role that could be found on a class such as the Warlock. Overall, Priests bring a valuable set of tools that make them an asset for any party or raid. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

2. Learn

  • VERIFIED Talents become available starting at level 10 and continue using a familiar structure, including one-point milestone talents at 11, 21, and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone. To change your talents, including Legacy talents, visit a class trainer in one of the major cities. Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Baseline Abilities

  • VERIFIED Fear Ward : This ability is now available to all Priests at level 20, regardless of race. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Devouring Plague : This ability is now available to all Priests at level 20, regardless of race. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Priest Racial Abilities : Each race of Priest has two spells unique to their Race. They have been fully re-evaluated, and new ones have been added for Gnome Priests: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Gnome: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Confounding Flash : Learned at level 10. Confuses up to 5 nearby enemies for 3 seconds. Effect broken by taking damage. 0.5 second cast time. 2-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Contingency Plan : Learned at level 20. Places a ward on a friendly target for 30 seconds. If that target’s health drops below 35%, the target is instantly protected by a damage absorb shield and gains a heal-over-time effect. These two effects can be stacked with Renew and Power Word: Shield. 5-minute cooldown. Instant cast. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Dwarf: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Desperate Prayer : Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Chastise : Learned at level 20. Damages and immobilizes a target for 2 seconds. Only useable on Humanoids. Instant cast, 2-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Human: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Divine Grace : Learned at level 10. Instantly heals a friendly target below 50% health and removes Weakened Soul from that target. Cannot be cast on self. 5-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Feedback : Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Night Elf: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Starshards : Still learned at level 10. Damage increased substantially but now has a 30 second cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Elune’s Grace : Still learned at level 20. Now reduces the chance for melee and ranged attacks to hit the Priest by 50% for 15 seconds or until 3 attacks have missed. 5-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Troll: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Hex of Weakness : Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadowguard : Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Undead [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Touch of Weakness : Unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Dark Sacrifice : Learned at level 20. Cannibalize your own health to gain Mana over time. Mana gain increased by Spirit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Fade : Threat reduction effect doubled. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Prayer of Healing : Instead of healing the Priest’s own party, this spell now heals all the members of the target’s party and their pets. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadow Word : Death: Added as a baseline ability to all Priests at level 32. Deals instant Shadow damage to the target but if the target is not killed by this spell, the Priest takes 10% of their max health in damage. 15 second cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Divine Spirit : Now baseline to all Priests, learned at level 30. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Renew : Renew can now be active from multiple Priests on the same target. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

4. Progress

Discipline

  • VERIFIED Power in Light : (New row 1 talent.) Increased Smite and Penance damage done to targets afflicted with your Holy Fire. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Wand Specialization : Now only requires two talent points for the same benefit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Twin Disciplines : (New row 1 talent.) Increases damage and healing done by instant cast spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Silent Resolve : Now also reduces the duration of Stun, Fear, and Silence effects on you. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Holy Precision : Increases chance to hit with Holy spells by up to 18%. This talent is intended to remove the need for hit chance on healing Priests, who are not expected to wear gear that increased hit chance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Power Word : Shield: Slightly more increase per talent point. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Mental Agility : Moved from row 4 up to row 3. Now costs only 3 talent points for the same effect and also benefits Smite and Holy Fire. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Meditation : Mana benefit from spirit increased substantially. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Inner Fire : Benefit to armor increased, and now also increases charges. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Mental Strength : Moved from row 4 up to row 4. Now increases Intellect by a larger percentage instead of Mana by a percentage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Soul Warding : This new 16-point gold medal talent reduces the cooldown on Power Word: Shield and its Mana cost. Requires Improved Power Word: Shield. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Mana Burn : Reduction to cast time of Mana Burn increased. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Penance : (New 21-point gold medal talent.) Channeled over 2 seconds with 12 second cooldown. Can target enemies to deal damage every second, or allies to heal damage every second. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Renewed Hope : (New talent.) Requires Soul Warding. Increases critical strike chance of Flash Heal, Binding Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, and Penance when casting on targets with Weakened Soul. Each of those spells also reduces the duration of Weakened Soul on the target each time the target is healed. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Divine Aegis : Critical heals create an absorb shield for 12 seconds on your target based on a percentage of the amount healed. This effect does not stack. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Unbreakable Will has been combined into Silent Resolve, Improved Power Word: Fortitude and Divine Spirit are now baseline abilities, Force of Will has been removed. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Holy

  • VERIFIED Twilight Focus : Formerly Healing Focus. Now grants pushback protection on all spellcasts, but costs 3 talent points instead of 2. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Blessed Recovery : Now also triggers from any attack that deals more than 30% of your maximum health in a single hit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Inspiration : Now triggers from all non-periodic critical heals. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Healing : Now also reduces Mana cost of Penance and Prayer of Mending. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Searing Light : (Redesigned.) Now increases all Holy damage done but by a lesser percentage. In addition, it now grants a chance for Holy Fire damage to make your next Holy Nova cost no Mana. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Binding Heal : (New 16-point gold medal talent.)  New spell with no cooldown. Heals the Priest and their current target. No cooldown, 1.5 second cast. Tuned to be as good as Flash Heal even if the Priest needs no healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Litany of Light : Each time you cast a healing spell, you are refunded part of its Mana cost if the name of the spell is different than the previous healing spell you cast. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Spiritual Guidance : Bonus to spell damage reduced to approximately one third as much as the bonus to spell healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Spiritual Healing : Now only needs 3 talent points for the same benefit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Prayer of Mending : (New 31-point gold medal talent.) Spirit of Redemption required. 10 second cooldown. Places a spell with 30 second duration on the target that heals the target the next time they take damage or receive non-periodic healing, consuming a charge. Then the spell jumps to another nearby party or raid member within 20 yards. It continues jumping until it heals a total of 5 times. Each target may only have one Prayer of Mending active. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Prayer of Healing, Lightwell. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Shadow

  • VERIFIED Shadow Focus : Moved to row 1 up from row 2. Instead of reducing the target’s chance to resist, it increases the chance for Shadow spells to hit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadow Affinity : Threat reduction to Shadow spells increased slightly. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadow Reach : Moved to row 2 up from row 4. Now requires only 2 talent points for the same effect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Psychic Scream : Blackout is now a pre-requisite. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Mind Flay : New row 3 talent. Requires Mind Flay. Increases the damage and range of Mind Flay but reduces its movement speed slowing effect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Vampiric Embrace : Moved to the row 3 as the 16-point gold medal talent. Now lasts 30 seconds with a 1-minute Cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadow Weaving : Now benefits only the casting Priest and not other party or raid members. Percentage increase to damage decreased moderately. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Silence : No longer requires Improved Psychic Scream as a pre-requisite. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Devouring Contagion : (New row 5 talent.) Reduces the mana cost of Devouring Plague, and when a target dies while afflicted with Devouring Plague, Devouring Plague will jump to a new enemy within 5 yards with the same remaining duration. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Early Demise : (New row 6 talent.) Increases Shadow Word: Death’s critical strike chance on targets below 20% health. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shadowform : Now allows all non-healing spells to be cast while in Shadowform, reduces the Mana cost of all Shadow spells by 50%, and increases the critical strike damage bonus of Shadow spells by 100%. Bonus to Shadow damage dealt decreased. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Vampiric Embrace. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Priests continue to be the most versatile healer in World of Warcraft which is identifiably their most important strength. While other healers focus on heal-over-time spells (HoTs) or direct heals, Priests can do both while having the unique capability of providing damage absorption shields which provide damage mitigation. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED As Discipline, they can lean even more into that strength with talents that improve upon Power Word: Shield. However, Priests are also a very vulnerable class with their limitation of wearing cloth armor and few ways to escape enemies.They instead need to rely on the support of friends with tanking or control abilities, while making those allies even stronger through their buffs and healing capabilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
