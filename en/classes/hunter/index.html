1. Understand

Official overview (quotes)

  • VERIFIED Taking Aim at the Hunter Class [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Hunters can focus their talents through Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, and Survival. In World of Warcraft: Forever, these specializations build around pet-focused play, ranged attacks, and trap and melee options while keeping the class’s familiar foundation intact. Here’s the current lineup of class abilities and changes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED These updates center on giving the class more flexibility while strengthening its identity as a ranged combatant with a trusted companion at its side. Aimed Shot is now baseline at level 20, traps can be used in combat, and pets gain more definition through Hunter gear scaling, broader ability access, and unique family abilities—including the addition of Foxes as a new pet family. Across Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, and Survival, talent updates support pet-focused play, ranged precision, and trap or melee-oriented build options. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Hunters have increased utility with traps now usable in combat but still can only place traps at their own location.  Hunters have an amazing toolkit of problem-solving Aspects and attacks but cannot Stun or Interrupt. And now their talent trees let them double down on aspects of their class like ranged combat and their pets, choose not to have a pet at all, or to add new skills to their arsenal that allow them to dance back and forth between ranged and melee combat. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Class description (official race & class guide): Hunters know the uncharted places of the world. Gifted with a deep empathy for all life, they have an uncanny knack for befriending wild animals and taming them as pets. In addition, hunters can use their finely honed senses to become master trackers. Wherever hunters go, they fight back the ravages of sickness, exploitation, and industrialization. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Specializations : Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, Survival [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

2. Learn

  • VERIFIED Talent trees follow that same direction. Starting at level 10, talents become available and keep the familiar structure, including the same number of rows and one-point milestone talents at 11, 21, and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone. Some talents remain exactly as they were in patch 1.12 (from original World of Warcraft), while others have been adjusted or improved, and several key class buffs that once came from talents, such as Blessing of Kings and Improved Mark of the Wild, are now baseline for their classes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

4. Progress

Beast Mastery

  • VERIFIED Deadly Aspects : (Formerly Improved Aspect of the Hawk.) This talent now also has a chance for melee attacks to grant increased attack speed while Aspect of the Beast is active. This supports more melee-oriented builds we will see in Survival. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Endurance Training : The old talent Thick Hide was merged with this one, so the talent now grants your pet Health and Armor. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Focused Fire : This new talent increases all damage you and your pet deal while your pet is active. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Aspect of the Monkey : Now grants twice as much Dodge, and your pet gains half the benefit of your Aspect of the Monkey. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Pathfinding : moved from row 3 to row 2 in the Talent Tree. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Unleashed Fury : Benefit to pet damage decreased slightly, but now also benefits new Hawk summons. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Mend Pet : Now also decreases the Mana cost of Mend Pet. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Ferocity : Critical strike bonuses to pets decreased, but now also benefits Hawk summons. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Summon Hawk : (New 16-point gold medal talent.) Deals instant damage and summons a Hawk to attack the Hunter’s current target for 18 sec. Hunter’s can have up to 2 Hawks active at one time. Cooldown shared with Arcane Shot. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Intimidation : Now requires Bestial Swiftness as a pre-requisite. Now also grants your pet 100% increased critical strike chance on its next attack. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Bestial Discipline : Now also grants the Hunter Mana regeneration from Spirit while casting spells (or spending Mana). [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Eyes of the Beast is now a baseline ability and Thick Hide has been combined with Endurance Training. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Marksmanship

  • VERIFIED Hawk Eye : Moved from row 3 up to row 1. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Lethal Attacks : Formerly Lethal Shots. Moved from row 2 up to row 1. Now grants increased critical strike chance to all attacks. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Stings : Formerly Improved Serpent Sting. Moved from row 4 up to row 2. Larger increase to Serpent Sting damage, and now also shortens the cooldown of Viper Sting and increases the duration of Scorpid Sting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Efficiency : Moved to row 2 down from row 1. Grants a larger Mana discount that now applies to Stings and melee abilities, as well as Shots. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Careful Aim : (New talent.) Increases melee and ranged Attack power by 20/40/60/80/100% of the Hunter’s Intellect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rapid Killing : (New talent.) Reduces cooldown on Rapid Fire and grants Rapid Killing when you kill an enemy or it dies while afflicted by your Serpent Sting. Rapid Killing increases the damage of your next Shot. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Arcane Shot : now reduces the cooldown of Arcane Shot by up to an extra 0.5 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Lone Wolf : (New 11-point gold medal talent.) Grants the Hunter 20% increased damage with all attacks while the Hunter has no active pet. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Trueshot Aura : Moved from row 7 up to row 4. Now the 21-point gold medal talent. New lower-level ranks added, but otherwise unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Mortal Shots : Now has pre-requisite of Careful Aim. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rapid Recuperation : (New talent.) Prerequisite of Rapid Killing. Now grants Mana regeneration from Spirit while casting after hitting a target with Serpent Sting, and even more during Rapid Killing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Barrage : Damage bonus reduced, but now also applies to Aimed Shot, as well as Multi-Shot and Volley. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Sniper Shot : New talent in 31-point gold medal slot. Pre-requisite of Trueshot Aura. Grants a new Shot with a 4 second cast time and 15 second cooldown that deals even more damage than Aimed Shot. Sniper Shot has a base range of 45 yards and increases the range of your next 3 Shots by 10 yards. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Hunter’s Mark and Aim Shot are now baseline abilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Survival

  • VERIFIED Improved Tracking : This new talent combines the Monster Slaying and Humanoid Slaying talents into a new improved talent that causes the Hunter to deal increased damage to any creature type the Hunter is currently tracking. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Entrapment : (Slight redesign.)  Now always triggers when the Hunter’s trap triggers, but the duration is increased by more talent points instead of the chance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Savage Strikes : (Redesigned.) Now increases all melee damage by 2/4% instead of Raptor Strike and Mongoose Bite by 10/20%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Survivalist : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Wing Clip : Now only takes 3 talent points to reach maximum 20% chance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Surefooted : Moved from row 4 up to row 3. Hit chance now applies to all attacks, and reduces the duration of movement impairing effects, instead of increasing their chance to miss the Hunter. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Survival Tactics : (Formerly Trap Mastery.) Now also increases the chance for Feign Death to hit, in addition to Traps. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Predator’s Edge : (New talent.) Increases melee critical strike damage bonus, and increases damage done by off-hand while dual-wielding. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Counterattack : Moved from row 5 up to row 4. Now the 16-point gold medal talent. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Resourcefulness : (New talent.) Reduces the mana cost of all Traps and melee abilities and grants your critical strikes a chance to allow Mana regeneration from Spirit while casting spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Expose Prey : (New talent.) Melee and ranged attacks on the target of your Hunter’s Mark have a chance to activate Mongoose Bite as if you had Dodged an attack. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Survivalist’s Discipline : (New talent.) Reduces the cooldown of Traps and Deterrence. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Strider Kick : New 21-point gold medal talent. An instant weapon damage attack that also increases your movement speed by 30% for 3 sec. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Lightning Reflexes : Agility bonus decreased to make room for new talent powers. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Lacerating Strikes : New 31-point gold medal talent. Mongoose Bite now also causes the target to Bleed over 21 seconds for damage based on the strength of the Mongoose Bite hit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Wyvern Sting, Killer Instinct, Improved Feign Death have now been combined into a new Resourcefulness talent, Monster Slaying and Humanoid Slaying have been combined into a new Improved Tracking talent. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Despite these changes, Hunters continue to feature unique strengths and weaknesses. Hunters only have one combat role, damage, but can utilize their pet as a tank in solo and small group play. Hunters have strong ranged abilities and the longest range of any class. But Hunters’ ranged abilities become unavailable in the “dead zone” within 8 yards of their enemy. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
