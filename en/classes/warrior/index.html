1. Understand

Official overview (quotes)

  • VERIFIED Forged in Battle : Warrior Class Updates [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Warriors charge into battle through Arms, Fury, and Protection, turning Rage, stances, and weapon mastery into momentum on the battlefield. In World of Warcraft: Forever, the Warrior continues to stand at the front line with updates that reinforce its familiar role as a durable, hard-hitting combatant while creating more room for each specialization to shine. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Warriors remain the masters of melee combat. They can both dish out melee damage and mitigate damage done to them. They have a unique strength in dealing sustained damage to multiple melee targets, found on no other melee class. They have a powerful toolkit to solve nearly any problem, but they lack the tools to easily recover from taking too much damage. And they have unique buffs and debuffs that only Warriors can supply. Any party or raid is happy to add a Warrior as either a tank or damage dealer. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

2. Learn

  • VERIFIED Talents become available starting at level 10 and continue using a familiar structure, including one-point milestone talents at 11, 21, and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone. To change your talents, including Legacy talents, visit a class trainer in one of the major cities. Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Base Class Abilities

  • VERIFIED First, we want to acknowledge that there are some bugs players are experiencing with Rage generation in the beta, and we are working on tracking down and identifying the root cause. It may take a couple weeks to track down as our engineers work through a variety of tasks. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rage: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rage from taking damage : We kept the original formula for Rage generation from taking damage, but with one big change. Rage gain from damage is calculated by ignoring Armor. So, wearing Armor will no longer reduce the Rage you gain. We are using the old formula as if you always had 50% damage reduction from Armor. Rage gain also ignores all damage absorption effects, so Power Word: Shield will no longer decrease Rage gained. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rage from dealing damage : We have fully-normalized this Rage. Rage gained is now based on the speed of the weapon that dealt the damage. In the launch beta, we gave no additional Rage for dealing critical strikes. We are re-adding that. Critical strike will now give 75% more Rage than non-critical strikes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Tactical Mastery : Learned at level 14. Allows the Warrior to keep 10 Rage when changing Stances. Can be further improved with Arms talents. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Victory Rush : Learned at level 20. 30-second cooldown instant attack that deals damage (increased by Attack Power) and heals the Warrior for 10% of maximum health. Activated for 20 seconds every time you kill a non-trivial enemy. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Recklessness : No longer shares a cooldown with Shield Wall and Retaliation. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Retaliation : No longer shares a cooldown with Recklessness and Shield Wall. Cooldown reduced to 15 minutes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shield Wall : No longer shares a cooldown with Recklessness and Retaliation. Cooldown reduced to 15 minutes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rend : Now gains significant damage scaling from Attack Power. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Thunder Clap : Now gains significant damage scaling from Attack Power. Useable in Defensive Stance. Cooldown increased to 6 seconds. Threat rebalanced (reduced) to account for being usable in Defensive Stance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Battle Shout : Attack Power bonus reduced to compensate for both factions having both Shaman and Paladin buffs. But benefit of Improved Battle Shout also baked in. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Sunder Armor : Now usable even if the target has a more powerful Expose Armor effect active. Threat generated now scales with Attack Power. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

4. Progress

Arms

  • VERIFIED Improved Tactical Mastery : (Formerly Tactical Mastery.) Each point now increases the Rage retained when shifting stances by 3 to a maximum of 25. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Overpower : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Anger Management : Tooltip clarified, but no functionality change. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Spearing Strike : (New 16-point gold medal talent.) An instant attack for 15 Rage with a 20 second cooldown. Deals a percentage of weapon damage to target and dismounts them. If the target was mounted, a Dragon, or a Giant, damage is doubled. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Bloodthrill : (New row 5 talent.) Your main hand attacks on enemies afflicted with Rend have a chance to activate Overpower for 6 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Weaponmaster : (New row 5 talent.) Combines the benefits of all weapon specialization talents into a single talent. You gain a different bonus based on your equipped weapon. Axes and Polearms grant increased critical strike chance. Maces and Staves ignore some of the target’s armor, and Sword have a chance to gain an extra melee autoattack. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Slam : Moved from Fury to row 6 of Arms and redesigned. Only 2 points. Reduces the cooldown, global cooldown, and cast time of Slam, and Slam no longer delays or interrupts your melee auto attack swing time. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Axe Specialization, Mace Specialization, Sword Specialization, and Polearm Specialization all removed. These are now a part of the Weaponmaster talent. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Fury

  • VERIFIED Booming Voice : No longer modifies Shout durations. That modification is now baseline. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Iron Will : Moved from Protection to row 2 of Arms. Now reduces duration of Stuns instead of increasing resistance to them. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Unbridled Rage : Chance increased. Now grants 2 Rage if the weapon equipped is a two-handed weapon. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Cleave : Now reduces the Rage cost of Cleave instead of increasing its bonus damage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Blood Craze : Now also triggered by attacks that deal more than 20% of your maximum health in a single hit and by striking a target with Bloodthirst. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Boundless Rage : New row 3 talent. Increases your maximum Rage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Dual Wield Specialization : Now also increases chance to hit with off-hand weapons. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Raging Blows : (New 16-point gold medal talent.) Causes your Whirlwind to hit with both weapons when dual-wielding and reduces the Rage cost of Cleave. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Enrage : (Redesigned.) Now has a chance to trigger from taking any damage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Precision : New row 5 talent. Increases chance to hit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Death Wish : Minor redesign. Now increases all damage taken while active instead of decreasing Armor and Resistances. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Flurry : Total increased attack speed reduced by 5%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Bloodthirst : Minor redesign. No longer heals the Warrior. Instead increases movement speed by 10% for 10 seconds. Damage retuned to have a base value and less scaling from Attack Power. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Demoralizing Shout and Improved Battle Shout are now Baseline abilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]

Protection

  • VERIFIED Shield Specialization : Now has chance on Blocks to generate 5 Rage instead of 1 Rage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Anticipation : Now grants twice as much Defense Skill per talent point. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Bloodrage : Now increases initial Rage and Rage over time, instead of just initial Rage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Master of Defense : New row 3 talent. Requires Shield Specializaton. Grants a chance to gain 5 Rage when the Warrior Dodges or Parries while a shield is equipped. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Revenge : (Redesigned.) Now increases damage done by Revenge instead of a chance to Stun the target. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Defiance : Now requires a shield equipped to increase threat in Defensive Stance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Disarm : Now reduces the cooldown on Disarm instead of increasing its duration. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Vanguard : New 16-point gold medal talent. Charge is now useable while in Defensive Stance. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Shield Wall : (Redesigned.) Now reduces the cooldown on Shield Wall by a large amount. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Bastion : New row 5 talent. Increases all damage done while you have a shield equipped. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Focused Rage : (New row 6 talent.) Reduces the Rage cost on all offensive abilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shield Slam : Damage scaling from Block Value increased. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed Talents : Improved Shield Block and Improved Taunt are now baseline abilities; One-Handed Weapon Specialization has been removed. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Priest and Warrior, 2026-09-30]
