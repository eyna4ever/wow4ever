1. Understand

Official overview (quotes)

  • VERIFIED Shifting Forms with the Druid Class [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Druids can shape their path through Balance, Feral Combat, and Restoration, adapting between spellcasting, shapeshifting, and healing as the moment calls for it. In World of Warcraft: Forever, the Druid remains rooted in versatility, with updates that support its many forms while keeping the class’s familiar connection to nature intact. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Their updates focus on making shapeshifting feel more fluid and useful across more of the game while strengthening each path through Balance, Feral Combat, and Restoration. Shapeshifted Druids can use more consumables, benefit from weapon effects, interact with more objects, and use racial abilities without leaving form, while key updates such as Nature’s Grasp and Omen of Clarity becoming baseline, Lacerate joining the Feral toolkit, and Revive giving Druids an out-of-combat resurrection spell add more flexibility across the class. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Druid continues to be an extremely versatile class, with the ability to serve any combat role, given the right equipment and talents. But that versatility comes at the cost of non-damage utility. Druids cannot snare targets, cannot Silence or Interrupt, and have weaker and less reliable crowd control abilities. While they can be powerful healers, their toolkit is more limited than Priests and relies on healing-over-time  (HoT) spells that require more planning. They are fully capable tanks and damage dealers, but lack the extended utility toolkits found on Rogues, Warriors, or Mages. Druids can perform any role but will need to rely on others to provide the complete toolkit needed for raids and dungeons. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Class description (official race & class guide): Druids live in a state of unparalleled union with nature. Tightly bound to the plant and animal kingdoms, they are natural shapeshifters, and so they know firsthand the abuse visited on their wild brethren. In consequence, despite their numbers, druids tend to be wary, reclusive, and difficult to spot. Few outsiders have plumbed the depths of their secrets. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Specializations : Feral, Restoration, Balance [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

2. Learn

  • VERIFIED Talent trees follow that same direction. Starting at level 10, talents become available and keep the familiar structure, including the same number of rows and one-point milestone talents at 11, 21, and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point milestone. Some talents remain exactly as they were in patch 1.12 (from original World of Warcraft), while others have been adjusted or improved, and several key class buffs that once came from talents, such as Blessing of Kings and Improved Mark of the Wild, are now baseline for their classes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Base Class Abilities

  • VERIFIED Shapeshifting: [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED All potions and almost all similar consumables are now useable while shapeshifted and do not cancel the Druid’s shapeshift. Rage potions and Thistletea are also now usable by Druids. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED One notable exception is Engineering bombs. Most cannot be used still, but some specific new recipes can be used while shapeshifted. See the SAF-T and EZ-Thro recipes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Weapon enchantments and procs now all work while shapeshifted, including effects like weightstones, Crusader enchant, and Windfury Totem. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED While Shapeshifted, the Druid’s melee auto attack DPS (Damage Per Second) while in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form is now the same as the DPS of the Druid’s equipped weapon. But the speed of attack is changed to 1.0 or 2.5 seconds, depending on the form. Abilities that deal weapon damage likewise use these damage values. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Most doors and objects can be opened and interacted with while shapeshifted without cancelling the Druid’s shapeshift. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Racial abilities : Now usable while shapeshifted. This includes Shadowmeld, War Stomp, and Walk on Air. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Tiger’s Fury : (Removed.) This has been replaced with a new Feral-specific talent called Shifting Power. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

4. Progress

Feral

  • VERIFIED Claw : Damage increased significantly to avoid the need for a new talented Combo builder to replace it. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Cower : Threat reduction effect doubled. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Demoralizing Roar : Now tuned to be approximately as effective as Demoralizing Shout, and identical to Demoralizing Shout at level 60. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Enrage : Now generates 10 initial Rage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Frenzied Regeneration : Now heals the Druid for 100% of max health over the duration (given sufficient Rage). [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Growl : Cooldown decreased to match Warrior Taunt cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Lacerate : This new baseline ability is now added at level 42. It applies a Bleed on the target dealing damage over 15 seconds and stacks up to 5 times. In addition, if the target is already afflicted by Lacerate, this ability deals 10% weapon damage per existing stack. Generates high Threat. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rake : Now gains increased damage from Attack Power. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shred : Damage increased significantly to need less talent support to be effective. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Swipe : Now gains increased damage from Attack Power. Still only usable in Bear Form and Dire Bear Form and strikes 3 targets. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Restoration

  • VERIFIED Revive : This new out of combat resurrection spell has been added at level 12. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Regrowth : Ability cost reduced significantly, basing Mana cost more on the upfront heal, and less on the later ticks of periodic healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Balance

  • VERIFIED Barkskin : Now usable while shapeshifted. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Entangling Roots : Now castable indoors. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Hurricane : Cooldown removed. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Faerie Fire : Now castable while shapeshifted into Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, costing no resources, but gains a 6 second cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Nature’s Grasp : Now baseline to the entire class, learned at level 10. Also, now castable while shapeshifted and while indoors. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Thorns : Now gains increased damage from the caster’s spell power, while they are present. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Wrath : Significantly redesigned to be a much cheaper Mana cost, but damage also lower significantly to match. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Omen of Clarity : Now baseline to the entire class, learned at level 20. Additionally, the Clearcasting effect is never consumed by Wrath (because Wrath’s Mana cost is so low). [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Talent Changes — Balance

  • VERIFIED Improved Wrath : This talent now also reduces the Mana cost of Wrath. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Genesis : (New talent in row 1.) Increases all periodic damage and healing done by the Druid’s spells and abilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Moonglow : Moved from row 5 up to row 2. Now reduces the Mana cost of all damaging spells but no longer modifies healing spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Moonfire : Now costs only 2 talent points instead of 5 for the same effect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Nature’s Majesty : (New talent in row 2.) Increases critical strike chance with all spells and melee attacks. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Nature’s Reach : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. And now also grants increased hit chance and applies to all your damaging spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Entangling Roots : Moved from row 2 down to row 3 and redesigned. Instead of preventing interruption, this talent now increases the damage done significantly and reduces the chance for other damage done to break the effect early. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Nature’s Splendor : New 11-point gold medal talent. Requires Nature’s Majesty. Increases the duration of Moonfire, Rejuvenation, Regrowth, and Insect Swarm. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Insect Swarm : New 16-point gold medal talent. Grants a new spell that deals Nature damage over 12 seconds and reduces the target’s chance to hit. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Vengeance : Now applies to all damaging spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Overgrowth : (New talent in row 5.) Allows the Druid to have up to 3 targets affected by Entangling Roots at once. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Nature’s Grace : Slightly redesigned. The effect now lasts 3 seconds and grants spellcasting haste and reduces your global cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Eclipse : (New talent in row 5.) Casting Wrath reduces the cast time of your next 2 Starfire casts, stacking up to 4 charges. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Moonfury : Now applies to all your damaging spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Moonkin Form : The increased party-wide critical strike chance from this talent now applies to all spells and attacks, not just spells. It is also now exclusive with the same effect from Leader of the Pack. Additionally, all non-healing spells can now be cast, instead of just Balance spells. Finally, while Moonkin Form is active, Omen of Clarity has double the chance to be triggered. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Nature’s Grasp is now a baseline ability, Natural Weapons has been incorporated into the Naturalist talent in Restoration, Natural Shapeshifter was moved to Restoration, Improved Thorns and Omen of Clarity are now baseline abilities. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Talent Changes — Feral

  • VERIFIED Ferocity : Now also applies to the new Primal Bite talented ability. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Heart of the Wild : Moved from row 6 up to row 1. Stat increase to Intellect and Strength reduced, but stat increase to Stamina in Bear Form unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Feral Swiftness : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. Movement speed bonus to Cat Form now works indoors and Dodge bonus applies all the time, not just in Cat Form. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Feral Instinct : No longer increases threat in Bear Form. Instead, increases damage done by Swipe. Stealth bonuses unchanged. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Brutal Impact : Now also reduces the cooldown on Bash. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Thick Hide : Redesigned. Now grants increased Armor while in Bear, Cat, Dire Bear, or Moonkin Form, and that increased Armor is based on Defense skill bonuses from buffs and equipped items. That increased Armor can be further multiplied by shapeshift-specific Armor multipliers. This causes Druid tanks to now gain benefit from Defense skill items similar to other tank classes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Savage Fury : Moved from row 5 up to row 3. Now also applies to Shred, but the damage increase is lower. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Feral Charge : This 11-point gold medal talent now also grants a version of this ability usable in Cat Form. In Cat Form the cooldown is 30 seconds and does not interrupt or immobilize the target. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Sharpened Claws : Now costs 2 talent points instead of 3 for the same effect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shredding Attacks : Formerly Improved Shred. Moved to row 3 up from row 4. Now decreases Shred Energy cost by more and also decreases Rage cost of Lacerate. This move in rows is not yet in beta and is new to next week’s build. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Shifting Power : New 16-point gold medal talent. Prerequisite Shredding Attacks. We are doing something a little different here, where there are two gold medal talents on this row, this one that is Cat-oriented, and Primal Bite that is Bear-oriented. Shifting Power instantly grants the Druid 40 Energy, but at a significant Mana cost. It starts with a 16 second cooldown, that can be further reduced by other talents. This talent is new to next week’s build and is not in the beta yet. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Primal Bite : The other 16-point gold medal talent, but for Bears. Melee attack for 20 Rage in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form that deals 100% weapon damage plus a bonus and generates very high threat. We have opted to go with this ability instead of Mangle. Requires Savage Fury as a prerequisite. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Blood Frenzy : The effects of the old Primal Fury and Blood Frenzy talent have been combined into this single 2-point talent. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Shifting Power : This new talent in row 5 requires Shifting Power and reduces the cooldown on Shifting Power down to 8 seconds. New talent not yet in beta. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Leader of the Pack : This 21-point gold medal talent was changed similarly to Moonkin Form. The critical strike chance bonus from this talent now applies to all spells and attacks and is exclusive with Moonkin Form. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Predatory Strikes : New talent in the row 5 that increases your critical strike damage bonus by up to 20%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Natural Reaction : New Bear-oriented talent in row 6 that increases Dodge chance and grants a chance to gain 5 Rage every time you Dodge. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Rend and Tear : New talent in the row 6 that increases damage your melee abilities deal to Bleeding targets. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Berserk : New talent 31-point gold medal talent inspired by its iteration in the original Wrath of the Lich King. This 15 second duration 3-minute cooldown ability is usable in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form. It clears and grants immunity to Fear, removes the cooldown on Primal Bite and makes it strike up to 3 nearby targets, and increases the critical strike chance of all Cat Form Combo abilities by 100%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Feral Aggression (Demoralizing Roar improvement now baseline), Primal Fury has been combined with Blood Frenzy, and Feral Faerie Fire is now a baseline ability. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]

Talent Changes — Restoration

  • VERIFIED Nature’s Focus : Moved from row 2 up to row 1. Now applies to interruption while casting all Arcane and Nature spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Furor : Cat Form portion of this redesigned. Conceptually, at 5 talent points it allows the Druid to regenerate Energy while not in Cat Form as if they were in Cat Form. But if they go to Bear Form, all that Energy is lost, to prevent “bear-weaving” gameplay we have seen in versions of Classic World of Warcraft. We realize this removed the “power shifting” gameplay some players prize, so we have re-added that gameplay in a much more approachable way with the Shifting Power talent in the Feral talent tree. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Naturalist : Formerly Improved Healing Touch. Now also increases all damage done by a percentage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Subtlety : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. Now also reduces all threat from Arcane and Nature spells, not just healing spells. Talent point cost reduced from 5 points to 3 points and for a maximum of 30% threat reduction. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Natural Shapeshifter : Moved from Balance tree to the row 2 of the Restoration tree. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Reflection : Mana regeneration while casting increased substantially. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Gift of Nature : Moved from row 5 up to row 3. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Gift of the Earthmother : (New 11-point gold medal talent.) Reduces the global cooldown on Rejuvenation, Swiftmend, and Wild Growth by 0.5 sec. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Swiftmend : Moved from row 7 up to row 4 as the 16-point gold medal talent. Redesigned to not consume the Rejuvenation or Regrowth effect on the target. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Living Spirit : (New row 5 talent.) Increases Spirit by a percentage. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Tranquility : Now also reduces the cooldown on Tranquility by up to 60%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Improved Regrowth : Now requires Improved Rejuvenation as a pre-requisite. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Wild Growth : New 31-point gold medal talent. Inspired by this ability from the original Wrath of the Lich King, it works slightly differently. It targets any party or raid member and then places a heal-over-time (HoT) effect on all members of that player’s party, including pets. The heal-over-time effect starts strong, and heals every 1 second for 7 seconds, decreasing the healing amount every second. It has a 6 second cooldown and a high Mana cost. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Improved Mark of the Wild and Improved Enrage are now a baseline abilities and Insect Swarm has been moved to the Balance tree. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Hunter and Druid, 2026-09-30]
