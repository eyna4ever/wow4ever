1. Understand

Official overview (quotes)

  • VERIFIED A Closer Look at Paladins [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Paladins offer one example of the wider class update philosophy : give each specialization new tools while preserving meaningful gaps in the class toolkit. Across the class, Paladins gain Holy Strike at level 6, an instant weapon strike that deals Holy damage on a 12-second cooldown. Seals are no longer consumed by casting Judgment, reducing the busy work of recasting them, while Seal of Fury gives Protection Paladins a tank-focused Seal that favors fast weapons, grants small absorb shields, and causes Judgment to taunt while the Seal is active. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Those updates give Paladins more tools, but they do not erase the class’s limits. Paladins still lack an interrupt, still cannot slow targets the way some other classes can, and still rely on the Seal and Judgment system to access their taunt rather than pressing a single-purpose button. They remain especially strong against Undead and Demons through spells such as Exorcism, Holy Wrath, and Turn Undead, while having fewer ranged options against other enemies. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED The goal is to make Paladins effective as tanks, healers, or Retribution melee damage dealers without smoothing away the edges that make the class feel distinct. More class and race details are planned through future videos and articles, giving players a deeper look at the vision for each class and specialization. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Paladin : Champions of the Light [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Our class deep dive series concludes with developer insights into Paladins in World of Warcraft: Forever. In this final installment, we’re looking at how the class is evolving through updates that build on its defining strengths: wielding Holy power, supporting allies, and standing firm against powerful foes. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Paladins remain rooted in their unique Seal and Judgement system, with new abilities and revised talents creating more room to shape how each specialization plays. Holy gains new tools for single-target healing, group support, and spell-focused damage; Protection receives stronger threat, mana, and survival options; and Retribution gains new ways to sustain Judgements, blend weapon attacks with Holy damage, and pursue a more approachable form of Seal Twisting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Paladins remain a distinct class with their own unique mechanics. Theyfeature the Seal and Judgement system, which our new talents play into and strengthen. They are very strong against Undead and Demons but have limited ranged capabilities. You can increase their range with Holy talents, but not to the extent found on our other mana users. They have no interrupt and no way to slow enemy movement. After all these changes, they can be highly effective tanks, competitive damage dealers, and the strongest single-target healers in the game. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Holy Paladins excel at sustaining heals on a single target with Flash of Light. Healing an entire party at once requires more planning, but new talents give them tools to step into that role when needed, making them viable healers across all forms of group play. Protection gains strong cooldown abilities with Swift Judgement and Templar’s Bulwark that ties directly to their iconic Seal, Judgement, and Forebearance systems, while baseline Consecration and talents like Iron Creed give them more consistent control of the battlefield. Retribution Paladins remain strong damage dealers with increased consistency and additional Holy power, bring significant group utility via talents like Sacred Arbiter maintaining judgements, and enjoy greater build flexibility to seal twist with Twist of Light or focus on Holy damage by hybridizing into Holy Shock. Overall, Paladins remain a strong class with distinct strengths and tradeoffs that preserve their unique identity, while bringing powerful flexibility and utility to every adventure. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Class description (official race & class guide): Paladins do battle to protect the world from the forces of shadow. Powerful warriors in their own right, they can also call upon the Light to heal wounds, create shields of force, and incinerate evil creatures. [Create the Hero You Want to Be in World of Warcraft: Forever, 2026-09-22]
  • VERIFIED Specializations : Holy, Protection, Retribution [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

2. Learn

  • VERIFIED Talents become available starting at level 10 and continue using a familiar structure, including one-point gold medal talents at 11, 21, and 31 points, now joined by a new 16-point gold medal. To change your talents, including Legacy talents, visit a class trainer in one of the major cities. Dual Specialization also becomes available at level 40. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]

General changes

  • VERIFIED Consecration is also baseline at level 20, serving as both a tanking and damage tool with high damage and threat on the first four targets it hits and lower damage and threat beyond that. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]

Baseline Abilities

  • VERIFIED Holy Strike : (New ability is learned at level 6.) Holy Strike deals instant Holy damage to the target based on 50% of equipped weapon damage plus a bonus that increases with each new rank. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Judgement : This ability no longer consumes the Paladin’s active Seal. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Salvation : This ability is no longer a Blessing, so a Paladin can now have a Blessing on the target as well as Salvation. The version of this spell designed for raids that targets an entire class is now called Mass Salvation. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Note : This change is not yet live and is going into a future beta build. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Righteous Fury : The threat bonus from this ability has been reduced. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Protection Paladins have access to more Holy damage than in Classic Era, so this multiplier does not need to be as high. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Seal of Fury : (New seal learned at level 10.) This seal has 3 components. It causes each of the Paladin’s melee swings to deal bonus Holy damage. That bonus Holy damage is constant per swing, unlike Seal of Righteousness, so this favors using a fast weapon while tanking. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Based on this bonus Holy damage, the Paladin gains an absorption shield equal to 50% of the damage. This absorption shield does not stack from multiple applications. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Finally, the Judgement effect from this Seal deals additional Holy damage and taunts the target to attack the Paladin. Because this taunt is delivered via Judgement, it has a uniquely longer 10-yard range; (Druid) Growl and (Warrior) Taunt have only a 5-yard range. This gives the Paladin a unique ability to “reach” enemies farther away than other tanks can. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Fire Resistance Aura, Frost Resistance Aura, and Shadow Resistance Aura: These auras now affect all raid members within 30 yards [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Consecration : This ability is now learned by all Paladins at level 20, instead of being a Holy talent. It has also been redesigned to prevent it from being too strong of a tanking tool. It now deals moderate damage to the first 4 enemies to enter it, and very low damage to all other enemies. This allows the Paladin to reliably generate threat on only 4 targets at a time, keeping them at parity with Warriors’ Thunderclap ability. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED This limitation is critical to maintaining the combat feel of Classic Era, and not sliding into the more AoE-focused gameplay found in early WoW expansions. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Lay on Hands : Cooldown reduced to 20 minutes from 1 hour. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Seal Twisting : This functionality no longer exists baseline like it did in Classic Era and Classic Anniversary. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED In World of Warcraft : Forever, we have brought in a new form of Seal Twisting via the Retribution 31-point gold medal talent Twist of Light. This capstone talent retains the engaging gameplay of swapping between multiple Seals while making it more intuitive and less stringent on timing windows. This allows the Paladin to engage in Seal Twisting while also having the flexibility to use their broad support kit more freely. Additionally, the Holy paladin gold medal talent Holy Shock has been strategically placed as an alternative to Twist of Light, allowing for players to opt out of Seal Twisting gameplay for a hybrid build utilizing Holy talents. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]

4. Progress

Holy

  • VERIFIED Holy Paladins gain tools that encourage more varied healing and support occasional damage-focused play. Improved Holy Strike reduces Holy Strike’s cooldown, Voice of Truth grants temporary immunity to Silence and Interrupts, and Reverence allows Paladins to regenerate Mana from Spirit while casting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Infusion of Light rewards Holy Shock and Flash of Light critical heals by reducing the cast time of Holy Light, encouraging healers to mix their spells rather than repeat the same cast. Holy Shock moves to a 10-second cooldown, Consecrated Ground causes enemies in Consecration to take increased Holy damage, and Light’s Vigil gives Paladins a high-cost cooldown that can reset Holy Shock and trigger either extra damage against enemies or party-wide healing through an ally. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Beta (Oct 15), replaces the Improved Holy Strike mention above: Improved Holy Strike removed from the Talent Tree. Holy Strike’s cooldown lowered to 10 seconds (was 12 seconds). [WoW Forever Beta Development Notes – Updated October 15 (Kaivax, official forums), 2026-09-24]
  • VERIFIED Healing Light : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. Now also increases Holy Shock healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Spiritual Focus : Now also applies to Light’s Vigil. Total talent cost reduced to 2 points from 5 points. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Improved Seals : (Formerly Improved (only)Seal of Righteousness.) Now affects damage from all Seals and Judgements. Total talent cost reduced to 3 points from 5 points. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Unyielding Faith : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. Now reduces the duration of Fear and Disorient effects instead of increasing chance to resist them. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Voice of Truth : (New 11-point gold medal talent that grants a new ability.) Voice of Truth grants immunity to Silence and Interrupt for 6 seconds, with a 3-minute cooldown. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Reverence : (New row 3 talent) Grants the Paladin up to 30% of their mana regeneration from Spirit while casting. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Purifying Power : (New row 3 talent) Reduces the mana cost of Cleanse and Purify by up to 20% and reduces the cooldown on Exorcism and Holy Wrath by up to 33%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED The Seal mana cost reduction helps with affording casting new Seals between melee swings to maximize damage. However, it will be challenging to afford the mana to sustain maximum use indefinitely. Utilizing abilities like Judgement of Wisdom and the Reverence Holy talent can help with sustaining this powerful effect [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Infusion of Light : (New row 4 talent.) Critical hits with Holy Shock and Flash of Light reduce the cast time of your next Holy Light by up to 1 second. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED In original World of Warcraft, Holy Paladins could end up relying heavily on repeatedly casting Flash of Light. This talent encourages greater healing variety while preserving their core strength in consistent single-target healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Illumination : (Requires Reverence.) This talent can now also be triggered by Light’s Vigil. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED In addition, it was historically too strong of an effect and scaled out of control, so the mana refund it grants has been reduced to 50% of the base cost of the affected healing spell. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Divine Favor : Now the 16-point gold medal talent instead of the 21-point gold medal talent. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Divine Precision : (New row 5 talent. Requires Holy Shock.) This talent grants up to 18% increased hit chance with Holy spells. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED We do not plan to include itemization for Hit chance on Plate healing gear, so this talent provides it instead. It further supports the possibility of the “Shock-adin” Paladin playstyle using Holy talents and gear. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Holy Shock : This ability has moved from the 31-point gold medal talent to the 21-point gold medal. Its cooldown has been reduced to 10 seconds from 30 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Moving Holy Shock earlier in the tree creates more flexibility for hybrid Shockadin builds while strengthening Holy Paladins’ core healing role. It also opens space for deeper talents such as Light’s Vigil, which builds on Holy Shock to provide new group-healing options without diminishing the specialization’s strength in single-target healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Consecrated Ground : This new row 5 talent causes your Holy spells to deal up to 10% increased damage to the first 4 enemies to enter your active Consecration. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Holy Power : Now increases the critical strike chance of Holy Shock and Holy Strike by up to 15%, while continuing to increase the critical strike chance of all other spells by up to 5%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Light’s Vigil : (Requires Holy Shock.) This new 31-point gold medal talent grants a new versatile ability to Holy Paladins. Light’s Vigil has a 1.5-second cast time, 30-second duration, and 6-second cooldown, and can be used on both allies and enemies. Regardless of which, the next Holy Shock cast on that target will not trigger its cooldown. If it is an enemy, they will take additional Holy damage and 75% of the mana cost of Light’s Vigil will be refunded. If it is an ally, every member of that ally’s party will be instantly healed for a moderate amount. Light’s Vigil can be active on only one friendly player per party. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED This ability further helps the “Shockadin” build while also providing a new tool for healing. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED When used proactively, a Holy Paladin can set up Light’s Vigil on several different parties in a raid group. And then when a large damage event happens to the entire raid, use Holy Shock back-to-back to heal the entire raid quickly. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED But it is more difficult to use reactively, as significant setup is required. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Altered talents : Improved Lay on Hands (cooldown reduced baseline) [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Talents Moved to Baseline : Consecration (now baseline), Improved Blessing of Wisdom (now baseline), Lasting Judgement (now baseline) [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]

Protection

  • VERIFIED Protection Paladins gain more ways to sustain Mana, recover from missed taunts, and survive dangerous moments without becoming a copy of another tank. Improved Seal of Fury grants Mana when Seal of Fury absorbs damage, Shield Specialization grants additional Mana on blocks, and Swift Judgment can reset Judgment once per minute so a missed taunt does not end the moment. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Templar’s Bulwark gives Paladins a powerful absorb shield based on maximum health while interacting with Forbearance, Reckoning has been redesigned to work for tanks who are immune to critical hits by also triggering from blocks, and Iron Creed lets timed Holy Strikes reduce incoming damage for a short window. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Toughness : Moved from row 2 up to row 1. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Redoubt : (Redesigned.) This talent no longer requires the Paladin to be hit with a critical strike; it now grants a 10% chance when hit to gain increased Block Chance. The increase to Block Chance is now up to 20% instead of 30%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Precision : Now increases chance for all spells and attacks to hit instead of just melee weapon attacks. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED By helping key abilities—including the taunt from Seal of Fury’s Judgement—land more reliably, this talent makes Protection Paladin tanking smoother and more approachable. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Anticipation : Moved from row 3 to row 2. Maximum increase to Defense doubled to 20 Defense, was 10 Defense. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Improved Seal of Fury : (New 11-point gold medal talent.) This talent improves the new tanking Seal to return mana to the Paladin each time one of Seal of Fury’s absorption shields is fully consumed. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED This mana increase is larger when the attacker is a higher level than the Paladin, such as when tanking more difficult content, including raid bosses. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Improved Righteous Fury : No longer increase threat from Righteous Fury. Instead, grants up to 6% reduced damage taken while Righteous Fury is active. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Shield Specialization : Now also causes your blocks to have up to 100% chance to restore 6% of your maximum mana. May only occur once every 3 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Sacred Duty : (New row 3 talent.) Increases total Stamina by up to 4% and reduces the cooldown on Divine Shield, Divine Protection, and Templar’s Bulwark by up to 1 minute. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Swift Judgement : (New ability.) New 16-point gold medal talent. No mana cost, 1 minute cooldown. Instantly finishes the remaining cooldown on your Judgement ability and reduces the mana cost of your next Judgement by 100%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED This new ability allows Paladin tanks flexibility to react to a resisted taunt, or even cleverly taunt multiple enemies quickly. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED One-Handed Weapon Specialization : Moved from row 6 to row 4. Now requires only 3 talent points instead of 5 for the full effect. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Templar’s Bulwark : (New 21-point gold medal talent.) This is a new instant ability with a 5-minute cooldown. You gain an absorption shield equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 seconds but applies Forbearance and cannot be used while Forbearance is active. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED We felt Protection Paladins needed a useful survival cooldown like other tanks have, and this fit the bill, while also making an interesting choice as part of their Forbearance system. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Reckoning : Now also gains up to 40% chance to trigger after Blocking a melee attack. It can still be triggered up to 100% of the time from taking a damaging critical hit. Cannot occur more often than once every 1.5 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED We wanted to make this talent useful to a tanking Paladin, who will often have reduced or no chance to be critically hit, so we added a chance from Blocking. But we don’t want to encourage tanking large numbers of enemies at once, so it cannot occur more often than once every 1.5 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED We’re aware that Reckoning currently does not let players save up 4 charges, and the triggered extra attacks can interrupt current auto-attack swing timers. We plan to fix these issues in an upcoming patch. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Iron Creed : (New row 6 talent.) Increases threat generated by Holy Strike by up to 15%, and while Righteous Fury is active, Holy Strike grants up to 10% reduced damage taken for 6 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED The intent of this talent is to increase the value of Holy Strike for both for threat generation and survival. A well-timed Holy Strike can help keep the Paladin alive at a critical moment in an encounter. And used rotationally, makes the Paladin a little bit more survivable on average. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Holy Shield : Now has Templar’s Bulwark as its pre-requisite. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Removed talents : Blessing of Sanctuary, Improved Concentration Aura [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Talents Moved to Baseline : Blessing of Kings. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]

Retribution

  • VERIFIED Retribution Paladins gain stronger melee identity while still leaving room for hybrid Holy damage options. Vindication now reduces enemy Attack Power while increasing the Paladin’s own, Sacred Arbiter empowers Holy Strike and refreshes Judgments on the target, Champion of the Light increases spell damage based on Intellect, and Instrument of the Law reduces threat generation while making Holy Wrath instant. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Twist of Light supports Seal Twisting without requiring a swing-timer add-on by letting a new Seal echo into the next melee swing, while players who prefer a different path can lean into Holy Shock for ranged, spell-powered play. [World of Warcraft: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap, 2026-09-13]
  • VERIFIED Deflection : Moved from row 2 up to row 1. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Benediction : Now reduces the cost of all instant cast spells and abilities by up to 10% instead of reducing costs on only Seals and Judgement by up to 15%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Holy Conduit : (New row 2 talent.) Reduces the mana cost of Consecration, Holy Wrath, and Exorcism by up to 40%. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Conviction : Moved from row 3 up to row 2. Tooltip wording updated to clarify that it benefits melee attacks specifically. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED Vindication : (Redesigned.) Now grants your damaging melee attacks a chance to reduce the target’s Attack Power, and to increase your own Attack Power by up to 3% for 30 seconds. [World of Warcraft: Forever Class Deep Dives — Paladin, 2026-10-15]
  • VERIFIED The Attack Power reduction is tuned by level to remain close to the reduction from Demoralizing Shout or Demoralizing Roar. Increasing the Paladin’s own Attack Power also ensures Vindication remains valuable against enemies that rely less on Attack Power, such as casters, or when the Paladin is not receiving most of the incoming damage.
