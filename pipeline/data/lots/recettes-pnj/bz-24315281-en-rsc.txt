36:T75c9,<p>Here you will find a list of hotfixes that address various issues related to World of Warcraft: Forever.</p>

<p>This list will be updated as additional hotfixes are applied.</p>

<h2><strong>Hotfixes</strong></h2>

<hr />
<p><strong><span>November 11, 2026</span></strong></p>

<p><strong>Classes</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Druid</strong>

	<ul>
		<li><strong>Feral</strong>

		<ul>
			<li>Rend and Tear now works on Pounce Bleed.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Hunter</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Fixed an issue where Volley was activating procs.</li>
		<li>Fixed an issue where Volley would continue to channel even when the Hunter had started moving.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Paladin</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Judgement changed to now be considered a Ranged Attack. This means it no longer counts as a Melee attack for the purposes of item and effect procs and uses the Paladin's Ranged Hit chance.</li>
		<li><strong>Retribution</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>With the fix to Windfury Totem activating from Holy Strike, we have changed Unsealed Fury. Unsealed Fury no longer generates echoes from Windfury Totem attacks.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Priest</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Confounding Flash (Gnome) can now correctly be used while in Shadowform.</li>
		<li><strong>Holy</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>The Twilight Focus talent will now correctly work while in Shadowform.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Shaman</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Fixed a bug where Flametongue Totem Rank 2 was not working.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Customer Support</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong> </strong>The Item Restoration service has been disabled while we investigate and address a critical bug.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Enemies and NPCs</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Riverglades - Significantly reduced the amount of damage dealt by the Twilight Enforcer's "Smash" ability.</li>
	<li>Riverglades - Lowered the damage dealt by Executioner Morg.</li>
	<li>Riverglades - Twilight Forgemaster's "Mark of Flames" now increases damage dealt by 50 (was 1000).</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Items</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Bloody Claws of Attack now correctly add +1 Fist/Unarmed Weapon skill.</li>
	<li>Earthen Silk Slippers no longer give more spell damage than intended.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Professions</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Tailoring</strong>

	<ul>
		<li>Adjusted the required materials and slot size of several bags that were unintentionally smaller than intended.
		<ul>
			<li>Red Mageweave Bag now requires 5 bolts of mageweave (was 4 bolts) and is now 14 slots.</li>
			<li>Green Silk Pack now requires 5 bolts of silk (was 4 bolts) and is now 12 slots.</li>
			<li>Black Silk Pack now requires 6 bolts of silk (was 5 bolts) and is now 12 slots.</li>
			<li>Green Woolen Bag now requires 5 bolts of wool (was 4 bolts) and is now 10 slots.</li>
			<li>Red Woolen Bag now requires 5 bolts of wool (was 4 bolts) and is now 10 slots.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Quests</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Western Plaguelands - Players should now be able to properly interact with the Command Tent while on the quest "Scarlet Diversions".</li>
	<li>Arathi Highlands - Fixed an issue where the Witherbark Mallet did not function, preventing players from proceeding to “The Giant in the Den”.</li>
	<li>Feralas - Fixed an issue where the Cage Door for the quest "Freedom for All Creatures" would not reset. It feels wrong to close it, but...</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>World</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li>Fixed a bug that prevented the ability to mount in named areas of Shen'dralas.</li>
</ul>

<hr />
<p><strong><span>November 10, 2026</span></strong></p>

<p><strong>Classes</strong></p>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Druid</strong>

	<ul>
		<li>Fixed an issue where Omen of Clarity could not activate after swapping specializations.</li>
		<li><strong>Restoration</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>Nature's Swiftness now also makes Wild Growth instant.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Hunter</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Fixed a bug where Pets erroneously had 150% of their normal health. This is now corrected to 115% of their normal health.</li>
		<li>Arcane Shot mana cost changed to 11% of base Mana for all ranks of Arcane Shot.
		<ul>
			<li><em>Developers’ notes: This means the Mana cost on all ranks will always be the same and rise gradually with level. Overall Mana cost at levels 6 and 60 are nearly identical to before this change, and slightly lower for levels in-between.</em></li>
		</ul>
		</li>
		<li><strong>Beast Mastery</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>Summon Hawk Mana cost changed to 11% of base Mana for all ranks of Summon Hawk, same as Arcane Shot.</li>
			<li>Melee damage dealt by the Hawk pet increased by about 80%.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
</ul>

<ul>
	<li><strong>Paladin</strong>

	<ul>
		<li>Redoubt can now be tracked in the Cooldown Manager.</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Priest</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Fixed an issue where the Revelation enchant was activated by Vampiric Embrace healing.</li>
		<li><strong>Holy</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>Fixed an issue where Holy Nova was not correctly unlearend when untalenting from the spell.</li>
		</ul>
		</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li><strong>Shaman</strong>
	<ul>
		<li>Maelstrom Weapon can now be tracked in the Cooldown Manager.</li>
		<li>Weapon imbues and Weapon totems can now be used at the same time.
		<ul>
			<li><em>Developers’ notes: For example, this means the Shaman can benefit from Windfury Weapon and Windfury Totem at the same time, granting their attacks a chance to activate both.</em></li>
		</ul>
		</li>
		<li><strong>Enhancement</strong>
		<ul>
			<li>Stormstrike damage reduced to 100% weapon damage (was 150%), and cooldown increased to 8 seconds (was 6 seconds).</li>
