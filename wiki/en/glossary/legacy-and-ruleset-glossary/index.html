In brief

Forever introduces terms that short notices cannot fully carry. This article fixes the meaning of Legacy Points and the move to rulesets, from official texts only.[1][2][3]

Key points

  • A Legacy Point is earned by completing a Legacy Challenge.[1]
  • Earned points and each character’s spending cap are separate ideas.[1]
  • No realm screen: you first choose a ruleset.[2]
  • Hardcore will use different Legacy rules.[1][2]

Legacy Points

Legacy Challenges work similarly to achievements in other WoW versions. Earned points feed perks and rewards. At launch Blizzard announces a spending cap of 16 points per character.[1]

Open the panel via the Legacy icon (shield) or the default hotkey Y once the first point is earned.[1]

Realmless play

Forever replaces realm selection with a ruleset choice. Each ruleset is a broad ecosystem, not one giant realm. See the dedicated guide for the Normal / PvP / RP / RP-PvP / Hardcore comparison.[2][3]

Hardcore case

Hardcore progress can carry elsewhere, but progress from other rulesets does not flow back into Hardcore. Exact Hardcore Legacy rules remain to be detailed when that mode ships.[2][1]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Legacy Points from challenges; spending cap 16 at launch.[1]
  • Rulesets instead of realms.[2]

Still to be confirmed

  • Full challenge and perk lists: see the official Legacy article; not copied here.
  • Hardcore date not announced.
