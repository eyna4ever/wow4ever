In brief

Forever announces more than a thousand new quests across levels 1–60. Alongside them, the Legacy system rewards account-wide challenges. Specific questlines, such as Lord Valthalak’s, are also documented.[1][2]

Key points

  • More than a thousand new quests announced for levels 1–60.[1]
  • Legacy usually unlocks at level 25, or via other thresholds (tradeskill, map).[2]
  • Announced launch cap: 16 Legacy Points spent per character.[2]
  • Additional 1–60 quests are planned after launch.[1]

New adventures 1–60

Blizzard sets Forever after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core. Players return to the original continents with new or reimagined stories.[1][3]

The Riverglades alone hold more than 150 new quests across much of levels 30–40.[1]

Legacy progression

Legacy is account-wide horizontal progression: challenges, perks and rewards. It opens with the first Legacy Point—often at level 25, or by reaching 150 in a non-gathering primary tradeskill, or by exploring the entire world map.[2]

Non-Hardcore characters share earned points but choose perks separately. The announced launch spending cap is 16 points per character.[2]

Lord Valthalak questline

A Lord Valthalak questline is recorded in the wiki’s documentary set with its sources. As with any specific line, only what those sources confirm is repeated.[4]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • More than a thousand new quests announced.[1]
  • Legacy system with a 16-point per-character spending cap at launch.[2]

Still to be confirmed
