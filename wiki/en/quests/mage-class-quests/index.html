    In brief

    Mage class quests: 51 quests in the beta quest index (to be confirmed). The class sheet: /en/classes/mage/.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Key points

    • 51 quests, 44 of them part of a chain.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • English names; objectives, rewards and givers: not included (index only).[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Quests (beta index, to be confirmed)

    The quest index consulted on 15 November 2026 (Forever beta 1.60.1 game data completed by player-addon contributions, file dated 20 October 2026) attaches 51 quests to Mage: 17 Alliance, 15 Horde, 19 for both factions. This data is not stamped with client 1.60.1.70862: to be confirmed in game.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
