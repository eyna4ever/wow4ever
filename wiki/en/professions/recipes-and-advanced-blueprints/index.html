    In brief

    Blizzard announces over 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges. You learn to craft your first campsite object at profession skill 20, from NPCs teaching camping basics or from profession trainers; more advanced objects come from blueprints dropped by certain dungeon bosses. The publisher has not released a complete recipe list, a named vendor, a named boss, or a drop rate.[1][2]

    Key points

    • First campsite object: skill 20, from starter-zone quests or purchase from a profession trainer. NPC names, quest names, exact zones and prices have not been published.[2]
    • Items requiring 140 or more: dungeon and raid drops (rule from the 21 October 2026 article). Official hotfixes create requirement exceptions: Tanning Rack at 125 (6 November) and Journeyman Campfire Kit at 90 (10 November). Loot sources for these items remain unpublished.[2][3]
    • Advanced Blueprint recipes drop from specific dungeon bosses, who are not named. No drop rate has been published.[1]
    • The Professions tab lists available recipes and blueprints, their materials, and whether crafting them raises your skill.[1]
    • Every profession has three camp objects, learned at different skill levels (only one example per profession is published in the official table).[1][2]

    What Blizzard has published about acquisition

    • Over 600 new recipes across professions and level ranges.[1]
    • First campsite object at skill 20: starter-zone quests, or purchase from a profession trainer. A short introductory quest also exists (its name is not published).[2]
    • Profession trainers are found in starting areas, capitals and around the world; none are named, and no coordinates have been published.[2]
    • Advanced blueprints: certain dungeon bosses (unnamed). Items at skill 140 or more: dungeon and raid drops — with the official requirement exceptions below.[1][2][3]
    • Cooks can learn upgraded campfires (5 or 10 objects). Alchemists, an Alchemy Lab; leatherworkers, a Tanning Rack.[2][1]
    • The Azeroth Commerce Authority and Durotar Supply and Logistics are “a major source of new tradeskill activities”: no recipe, quartermaster or standing has been published.[4]
    • Trade supply vendors exist on Zephras Isle (5 November hotfix: they did not sell apprentice tradeskill bags). They are not named, and the hotfix does not attribute recipes to them.[3]
    • Waylaid Crates and Craftsman’s Writs drop inside dungeons (9 November hotfix). The official text does not say they are recipes, nor which dungeons they come from.[3]

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Over 600 new recipes; first campsite object at skill 20 (starter-zone quests or a profession trainer); advanced blueprints from certain unnamed dungeon bosses.[1][2]
    • The 11 official table examples, plus the Alchemy Lab, upgraded campfires and the Tanning Rack (skill 125 since 6 November).[2][1][3]
    • Official EN names without published acquisition: Journeyman Campfire Kit (Cooking 90), Arcane Salvager, Fishing Rack, Recipe: Elixir of Holy Power, and the five tailoring bags adjusted on 11 November.[3]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Complete official list of recipes and blueprints for all professions: not published by Blizzard.
    • Named vendor NPCs, named trainers, exact zones, purchase prices, reputation requirements: not officially published (two vendors from the game files above).
    • Drop rates: not given on the 55 pages consulted, and not officially published. No chest or container object was listed on those pages.
    • “Quest”, “reputation”, “probably trainer” recipes and those with no recorded source: outside this pass (item pages not opened one by one).
