    In brief

    The Cooking Fire becomes a Basic Campfire around which nearby players add profession items. Blizzard publishes one example feature per profession; these effects are variants of class buffs and do not stack with them.[1]

    Key points

    • Basic Campfire: up to 3 features, one per player.[1]
    • Sitting or crafting at the campsite for at least 1 minute grants the other features’ benefits.[1]
    • All features require a campfire and share a 1-hour cooldown.[1]

    How to obtain

    • A short quest introduces the system early on (its name is not published).[1]
    • At skill 20: first items from starter-zone quests or from a profession trainer.[1]
    • Items requiring 140 or more: dungeon and raid drops.[1]
    • Official requirement exceptions, with no new acquisition source published: the Tanning Rack requires 125 (was 140) as of 6 November 2026; the Journeyman Campfire Kit is placed at Cooking 90, matching its recipe skill requirement (was 140), as of 10 November.[3]
    • Cooks later learn upgraded campfires (5 or 10 objects); other professions unlock e.g. Alchemy Laboratories and Tanning Racks.[1]

    Official November 2026 hotfixes (camp objects and named recipes)

    • 6 November: Tanning Rack skill 125 (was 140), so it no longer blocks leatherworking patterns that require 125; Tanning Rack skill-ups lowered by 15.[3]
    • 10 November: Journeyman Campfire Kit requires Cooking 90 to place (was 140), matching its recipe skill requirement. No official French name published (French hotfix page: 404 on 12 November).[3]
    • 10 November: Arcane Salvager is crafted with a Runed Golden Rod (was Runed Truesilver Rod) and correctly applies its disenchanting bonus to lower-level enchanters.[3]
    • 10 November: some Fishing Racks failed to provide lures; other “There is no loot” issues are acknowledged. This confirms the object’s existence.[3]

    These hotfixes do not say where the Tanning Rack, the kit, Arcane Salvager or the Fishing Rack are obtained (vendor, monster, chest or quest: not published).[3]

    Restrictions and risks

    • Effects are exclusive with the matching class buff (see table).[1]
    • Faction Banner: only members of your faction benefit.[1]
    • Camp Tent: no effect if rested experience already exceeds 5% of a level.[1]
    • On a PvP ruleset, the risk increases when enemy players find your camp.[1]

    Related Legacy Perks: Field Guide reduces the 1-hour cooldown, Permanence lengthens camping buffs and Reagent Economy removes materials from tier 1 features.[2]

    Correction history

    • 12 November 2026 (draft, not approved): official requirement exceptions (Tanning Rack 125, Journeyman Campfire Kit 90) and objects named in the 6 and 10 November hotfixes added. This revision is not a validation by Mathieu and not a publication.[3]

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • The 11 items in the table, their skill level and effect.[1]
    • Rules: 3 features, 1 per player, 1 minute, 1-hour cooldown.[1]
    • Hotfixes: Tanning Rack at 125 (6 Nov); Journeyman Campfire Kit at Cooking 90 (10 Nov); existence of Arcane Salvager and Fishing Rack (10 Nov).[3]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Where the Tanning Rack, Journeyman Campfire Kit, Arcane Salvager and Fishing Rack are obtained: not published.
    • Full list of each profession’s camp items, their materials and recipes: not published by Blizzard.
    • Exact buff duration: only the Sharpening Wheel is described as lasting “an hour”.

    Key dates

    1. 21 October 2026Official Camping article published.[1]
    2. 6 November 2026Hotfix: Tanning Rack skill 125 (was 140).[3]
    3. 10 November 2026Hotfixes: Journeyman Campfire Kit (Cooking 90), Arcane Salvager, Fishing Rack.[3]
