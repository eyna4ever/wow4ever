    In brief

    Leather, hides and scales: the 64 materials of this family used by Forever profession recipes, each with where to find it, what it is used for and which quests ask for it. Names, recipes, reagents and icons: game files (client 1.60.1.70862). Vendors, observed loot and quests: unofficial Forever database, to be confirmed.[2][4][3] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Key points

    • 64 materials, 25 with at least one source: 12 crafted by a game-file recipe, 2 sold by an NPC, 13 seen as loot, 9 tied to a quest (vendors, loot and quests to be confirmed).[2][4][3] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • 14 materials are asked for by at least one quest (to be confirmed).[3] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Which creatures to skin in Forever: generally not given. Only loot observed on some creatures is listed, to be confirmed.[5] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • 2 items with no readable name in the game files: only the item number is shown.[2] Game data (client 1.60.1.70862)

    How to read this list

    Each card says where to find the material, what it is used for and which quests ask for it. French and English names, quality, item level, crafting recipes, reagents, the professions using it, vendor sell price and icon come from the game files (client 1.60.1.70862). Vendors and their prices, observed loot, quests and positions (x, y in % of the zone map) come from an unofficial Forever database: they are marked “to be confirmed”. When information is missing, the card says “not given”: nothing is added from memory or from another version of the game.[2][4][3] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Vendors: the database consulted lists mostly Horde vendors (Orgrimmar, Durotar, The Barrens, Undercity, Thunder Bluff…). Alliance vendors are almost all missing: no vendor on a card does not prove nobody sells it. German names found in the source are flagged as such.[4] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
