Camping and crafting: the cooking fire becomes a shared campsite

The camping system turns the cooking fire into a shared camp. Up to three crafted objects provide services there; equivalent class buffs do not stack.

In brief

In Forever, the cooking fire becomes a Basic Campfire. Once placed it creates a shared campsite where nearby players can rest and benefit from crafted camp features before moving on. Blizzard wants those pauses to become useful encounters.[1][2]

Key points

  • A Basic Campfire supports up to three special crafted objects.[1]
  • Each nearby player may add one.[1]
  • Examples: sharpening wheel (Strength), faction banner (Spirit), herbalist incense.[1]
  • Advanced recipes and blueprints are announced separately.[2]

How camping works

The system expands Cooking’s cooking fire. Once placed, the Basic Campfire creates a shared space. Camp features give professions a more visible role in the world.[1]

  • A blacksmith can place a Sharpening Wheel that increases Strength for an hour.[1]
  • A tailor can place a Faction Banner that increases Spirit.[1]
  • An herbalist can place incense.[1]

Equivalent class buffs do not stack with these camp objects.[1][2]

Recipes and advanced blueprints

Blizzard also announces advanced recipes and blueprints for crafting. The wiki’s short notices summarize them without inventing unpublished reagent lists.[2]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Basic Campfire + up to three crafted objects.[1]
  • No stacking with equivalent class buffs.[1]

Still to be confirmed

  • The full per-profession camp-object list is not in the article consulted.
  • Exact durations and values beyond the cited examples remain to confirm article by article.
