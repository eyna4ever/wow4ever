    How to read this list

    Each card says where to find the material, what it is used for and which quests ask for it. French and English names, quality, item level, crafting recipes, reagents, the professions using it, vendor sell price and icon come from the game files (client 1.60.1.70862). Vendors and their prices, observed loot, quests and positions (x, y in % of the zone map) come from an unofficial Forever database: they are marked “to be confirmed”. When information is missing, the card says “not given”: nothing is added from memory or from another version of the game.[2][4][3] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Vendors: the database consulted lists mostly Horde vendors (Orgrimmar, Durotar, The Barrens, Undercity, Thunder Bluff…). Alliance vendors are almost all missing: no vendor on a card does not prove nobody sells it. German names found in the source are flagged as such.[4] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
