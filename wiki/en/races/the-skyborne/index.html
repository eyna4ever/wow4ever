In brief

The Skyborne are World of Warcraft: Forever’s new playable race. They begin on Zephras Isle (levels 1–12). Choosing the Horde (Windshaper) or the Alliance (High Order) unlocks Shaman or Mage, on top of the shared classes.[1][2]

Key points

  • Start: Zephras Isle, levels 1–12.[1]
  • Horde (Windshaper): Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman, Warrior.[1]
  • Alliance (High Order): Druid, Hunter, Mage, Rogue, Warrior.[1]
  • Ancestry: Highborne exiles bound to elemental wind spirits, with a civilization in Skywall.[1]

Origin and crisis

Thousands of years ago, Highborne exiles forged a pact with elemental wind spirits and built a civilization within Skywall. On Zephras that civilization is in crisis: the air spirits have vanished, ancient structures are failing, and communities are divided over what comes next.[1]

Both branches also share new Skyborne druid forms, racials, customizations, voices and their own dance.[1][2]

Access and upgrades

Access to the race and the Zephras starting experience is included in the Skyborne Heroic and Epic Packs and in the Warcraft Forever Collection.[3]
