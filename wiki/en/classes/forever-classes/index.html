The nine classes of World of Warcraft: Forever

In brief

Forever ships nine classes. Blizzard publishes per-class deep dives. This wiki only summarizes what those articles state: talent paths, design intent and announced changes. No invented statistics.[1][2][3][4][5]

Key points

  • Nine playable classes at launch.[1]
  • Each preview details talent paths and design goals.[2][1]
  • Skyborne only unlock Shaman on Horde and Mage on Alliance.[6]
  • A FR/EN contradiction on Multi-Shot / Aimed Shot is logged and kept out of the notices.[1]

Classes covered by the previews

Official previews exist notably for Hunter, Priest, Mage/Warlock-related coverage, Rogue and Paladin. Other classes are playable; this wiki does not invent detail the sources omit.[1][2][3][4][5]

This wiki’s method

Each short class notice cites its URLs. Long articles do not copy number tables the sources do not provide. Where language versions contradict, the disputed detail is left out.[1]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Nine classes at launch.[1]
  • Official previews published for several classes from late September to mid-October 2026.[1][5]

Still to be confirmed

  • Not every class yet has the same level of public detail.
  • The priest notice (CL3) remains a draft to correct: wording about the beta.

Source discrepancies

  • FR/EN contradiction on Multi-Shot and Aimed Shot in article 24301515; excluded from notices.[1]

Key dates

  1. 30 September 2026Hunter and priest previews.[1][2]
  2. 8 October 2026Mage/warlock and rogue previews.[3][4]
  3. 15 October 2026Paladin preview.[5]
