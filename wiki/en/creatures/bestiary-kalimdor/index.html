    In brief

    This page gathers the 1273 NPCs that the Forever list attaches to Kalimdor overworld zones (including capitals) — unofficial database, to be confirmed. It is not an inventory of every mob in those zones. Official creatures of Durotar or Teldrassil missing from the list stay on the official-notes sheet.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    Key points

    • 1273 NPCs across 21 Kalimdor zones, according to the list consulted.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • This is not a census of every mob: only NPCs from the Forever list are included (unofficial database, to be confirmed).[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    How to read this list

    Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary for World of Warcraft: Forever. Official names already covered on this wiki (hotfixes, Onyxia, the fox family) stay on their sheets: creatures in official notes (/en/creatures/creatures-official-notes/), Onyxia (/en/creatures/onyxia/), foxes (/en/creatures/foxes/), documented creatures (/en/creatures/documented-creatures/). These pages add the 3,284 NPCs from the Forever database consulted on 12 November 2026.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    • Every row from the NPC list comes from the unofficial Forever database (to be confirmed). This is not an official Blizzard confirmation. No official French name appears in that list: the English name is kept.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Type (beast, humanoid, demon…), normal/elite/rare classification, faction, sub-zone and a “skinnable” flag are not in this list: the Type column stays “not given”. An NPC is classed as “boss” only when the list marks it so (tag x). There is no “rare” tag in the list.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Role follows the list tags: boss, vendor, quest giver, quest objective, loot flagged. The absence of a “loot flagged” tag does not mean the NPC has no loot.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Recipes already documented on Recipes and advanced blueprints are recalled, with a link to that sheet, and without drop rates.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
