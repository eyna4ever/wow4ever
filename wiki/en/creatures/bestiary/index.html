    In brief

    Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary. This wiki gathers first the creatures already named in official texts (dedicated sheet: /en/creatures/creatures-official-notes/), then the 3284 NPCs from the Forever database consulted on 12 November 2026, each row from that list coming from the unofficial Forever database (to be confirmed). Regular mobs missing from that list are not invented.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    Key points

    • 3284 NPCs in the list consulted, across 76 zones after merging identical labels.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • 91 bosses tagged in the list (plus Onyxia, official, not tagged as a boss in this list). No “rare” tag.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]
    • Type, elite, faction, sub-zone and skinning: not given in this list.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    How to read this list

    Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary for World of Warcraft: Forever. Official names already covered on this wiki (hotfixes, Onyxia, the fox family) stay on their sheets: creatures in official notes (/en/creatures/creatures-official-notes/), Onyxia (/en/creatures/onyxia/), foxes (/en/creatures/foxes/), documented creatures (/en/creatures/documented-creatures/). These pages add the 3,284 NPCs from the Forever database consulted on 12 November 2026.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    • Every row from the NPC list comes from the unofficial Forever database (to be confirmed). This is not an official Blizzard confirmation. No official French name appears in that list: the English name is kept.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Type (beast, humanoid, demon…), normal/elite/rare classification, faction, sub-zone and a “skinnable” flag are not in this list: the Type column stays “not given”. An NPC is classed as “boss” only when the list marks it so (tag x). There is no “rare” tag in the list.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Role follows the list tags: boss, vendor, quest giver, quest objective, loot flagged. The absence of a “loot flagged” tag does not mean the NPC has no loot.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Recipes already documented on Recipes and advanced blueprints are recalled, with a link to that sheet, and without drop rates.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Scope and Forever evidence

    The list is published as the WoW Forever NPC database (3,284 NPCs with in-to be confirmed). It includes zones unique to Forever (Zephras Isle, Shen’dralas, Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of Dalaran). The same unofficial database, on its item database, claims to be drawn from WoW Forever client build 1.60.1.70862, as of 7 November 2026. The NPC hub page itself does not repeat that build number.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    Not included as rosters: official Forever dungeons with no NPC in this list (The Drowned City, Krol’dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold, The Shaper’s Terrace), and official raids still without a roster in the unofficial database (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit — Onyxia has an official sheet). Riverglades and Mount Hyjal have no NPC in this list. Two database label were grouped with a note: “Insel Zephras” with Zephras Isle, “The Hall of Thanes” with Hall of Thanes, “Temple of Ahn'Qiraj” with “Ahn'Qiraj Temple”. “Dalaran” (4 NPCs) was not merged with City of Dalaran (9 bosses).[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary for World of Warcraft: Forever; its official texts do name some creatures and the nine new dungeons, as well as the raids of 9 December 2026.[1][2][3][5]
    • The nine new dungeons and the 9 December 2026 raids (Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia’s Lair) are officially named, with level ranges for the new dungeons.[1][2]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Type, elite, rare, faction, hostility, sub-zone and skinnable flag: not given in the NPC list consulted.
    • The French NPC list uses the same English names as the English list (no official French names in that file).
    • Unnamed creatures or regular mobs missing from these 3,284 rows are not listed here.
    • The NPC hub is labelled Forever and contains Forever-only zones, but it does not repeat build number 1.60.1.70862, which appears on the item database in the same documentation.
