    In brief

    Blizzard announced three raids for 9 December 2026: Barrow Deeps (10), Hyjal Summit (20) and Onyxia’s Lair (40). No roster for those three is compiled here except Onyxia, already documented. This page also lists the 70 NPCs that the Forever list attaches to Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Zul’Gurub, Ahn’Qiraj and Naxxramas (unofficial database, to be confirmed).[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    Key points

    • Three official raids open on 9 December 2026: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit, Onyxia’s Lair.[1][2][3][5]
    • 70 NPCs attached to classic-named raids present in the unofficial database (unofficial database, to be confirmed).[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    How to read this list

    Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary for World of Warcraft: Forever. Official names already covered on this wiki (hotfixes, Onyxia, the fox family) stay on their sheets: creatures in official notes (/en/creatures/creatures-official-notes/), Onyxia (/en/creatures/onyxia/), foxes (/en/creatures/foxes/), documented creatures (/en/creatures/documented-creatures/). These pages add the 3,284 NPCs from the Forever database consulted on 12 November 2026.[1][2][3][5][6][7][8]

    • Every row from the NPC list comes from the unofficial Forever database (to be confirmed). This is not an official Blizzard confirmation. No official French name appears in that list: the English name is kept.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Type (beast, humanoid, demon…), normal/elite/rare classification, faction, sub-zone and a “skinnable” flag are not in this list: the Type column stays “not given”. An NPC is classed as “boss” only when the list marks it so (tag x). There is no “rare” tag in the list.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Role follows the list tags: boss, vendor, quest giver, quest objective, loot flagged. The absence of a “loot flagged” tag does not mean the NPC has no loot.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Recipes already documented on Recipes and advanced blueprints are recalled, with a link to that sheet, and without drop rates.[6][7][8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Blizzard does not publish a complete bestiary for World of Warcraft: Forever; its official texts do name some creatures and the nine new dungeons, as well as the raids of 9 December 2026.[1][2][3][5]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Type, elite, rare, faction, hostility, sub-zone and skinnable flag: not given in the NPC list consulted.
    • The French NPC list uses the same English names as the English list (no official French names in that file).
    • Unnamed creatures or regular mobs missing from these 3,284 rows are not listed here.
    • The labels “Ahn'Qiraj Temple” and “Temple of Ahn'Qiraj” were grouped. Blizzard has not, in the sources cited here, confirmed the in-game status of Molten Core, Blackwing Lair, Zul’Gurub, Ahn’Qiraj or Naxxramas in Forever.
