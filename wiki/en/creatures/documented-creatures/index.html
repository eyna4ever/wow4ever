    In brief

    Official Forever sources say little about creatures outside quest, raid or pet context. To avoid inventing a bestiary, this article sticks to Onyxia and the fox family, already in the documentary set.[1][2]

    Key points

    • Defeating Onyxia is listed among raid-category Legacy Challenges.[1]
    • Onyxia is also the December 9 40-player raid boss in Dustwallow Marsh.[2]
    • A fox pet family is documented for Hunters.[3]
    • No other complete official bestiary appears in the sources consulted.[1]

    Onyxia

    Onyxia’s Lair unlocks on December 9, 2026 for 40 level-60 players after earning the Drakefire Amulet. Defeating Onyxia is also cited as a Legacy Challenge.[2][1]

    Fox family

    The Hunter preview documents a fox pet family. Beyond that mention, this wiki does not add unpublished pet statistics.[3]

    Documentary limits

    Until Blizzard publishes more, the creatures category stays intentionally short. A thin category beats invented notices.[1]

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Onyxia: 40-player raid on December 9 + Legacy Challenge.[2][1]
    • Fox pet family mentioned for Hunters.[3]

    Still to be confirmed

    • No extended official bestiary in the 24–27 October 2026 sources.
    • Exact fox-pet abilities not detailed here.

    Key dates

    1. 23 September 2026Legacy Challenges (including raids) presented.[1]
    2. 20 October 2026Onyxia raid overview.[2]
    3. 9 December 2026Onyxia’s Lair unlocks.[2]
