    In brief

    Gnomeregan: dungeon present in the unofficial Forever database (to be confirmed) — 6 tagged bosses level 30–34, 6 NPCs. Blizzard has not published a dedicated sheet; Forever’s dungeon XP rules apply.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Key points

    • 6 tagged bosses in the unofficial Forever database (level 30–34), 6 NPCs in all.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Dungeon XP: +50% on November 8; since November 14, nullified only when a member is more than 12 levels above the enemies.[4][5][3][2][7][8]

    Bosses (unofficial database, to be confirmed)

    The NPC list of the unofficial Forever database attaches 6 NPCs to Gnomeregan, 6 of them tagged “boss”, level 30–34. Encounter order is not given.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    What Blizzard says

    Official hotfix of November 13, 2026: Techbot’s respawn time reduced for the quest “Save Techbot’s Brain!”.[2]

    Experience rules for every Forever dungeon, according to Blizzard: on November 8, dungeon enemy XP was raised by 50% (from 40% to 60% of Classic Era values, per Kaivax on November 10). On November 13, killing an enemy awarded no XP to the party if the enemy was trivial to any member; on November 14 (Pacific time), Blizzard relaxed the rule: XP is nullified only when the high-level player is more than 12 levels above the enemies. Instances already underway when it was deployed are not affected.[4][5][3][2][7][8]

    Information not given

    • Recommended level range, entrance location, group size: not given by Blizzard for Forever; boss levels above come from the unofficial Forever database.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Boss loot tables, item stats and drop rates: not given (Classic tables are not reused).[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • French NPC names: not in the list consulted; English names are kept.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Correction history

    • November 15, 2026: sheet created (draft) from the client 1.60.1.70862 NPC list, recipes already published, the beta quest index (to be confirmed) and official hotfixes.[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • 17 November 2026 (v42): NPCs, bosses, levels and recipes taken from an unofficial Forever database are now marked “to be confirmed” (they were wrongly shown as “game data”).[1] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Experience rules for every Forever dungeon, according to Blizzard: on November 8, dungeon enemy XP was raised by 50% (from 40% to 60% of Classic Era values, per Kaivax on November 10). On November 13, killing an enemy awarded no XP to the party if the enemy was trivial to any member; on November 14 (Pacific time), Blizzard relaxed the rule: XP is nullified only when the high-level player is more than 12 levels above the enemies. Instances already underway when it was deployed are not affected.[4][5][3][2][7][8]
    • Official hotfix of November 13, 2026: Techbot’s respawn time reduced for the quest “Save Techbot’s Brain!”.[2]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Recommended levels, entrance, boss loot and rates: not given; quests from a beta index, to be confirmed.
