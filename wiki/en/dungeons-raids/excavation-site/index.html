    In brief

    Five-player dungeon, levels 24–29, near Thelgen Rock and Dun Algaz in the Wetlands. Beyond Whelgar’s Excavation, a strange mist hides a place frozen in time, guarded by errant titan constructs and dangerous wildlife.[1]

    Key points

    • Levels 24–29 — Near Thelgen Rock and Dun Algaz, Wetlands.[1]
    • Blizzard publishes no faction-specific access note.[1]
    • Beyond the dig: a place frozen in time, guarded by errant titan constructs, that draws the Explorers’ League.[1]
    • 14 quests attached to this zone in the beta quest index (to be confirmed).[8] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Access, levels and location

    Access sheet (source: Blizzard, 2026-10-20)[1]
    LevelsLocationAccess
    24–29Near Thelgen Rock and Dun Algaz, WetlandsBlizzard publishes no faction-specific access note.

    Five-player dungeon, one of the nine new WoW: Forever dungeons (levels 13–60, Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor).[1]

    What Blizzard says

    A strange mist drifts down from the mountains above Whelgar’s Excavation, with flashes of light on the horizon. Beyond the dig lies a place frozen in time, guarded by errant titan constructs and dangerous wildlife — enough to draw the Explorers’ League.[1]

    Loot: what is confirmed

    Blizzard says every dungeon drop has been re-examined and hundreds of new drops added; unique dungeon boss items are now blue (Rare) quality and set bonuses have been improved. When a bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare item drops in a dungeon, its appearance is granted to every eligible looter.[3]

    Advanced camp feature blueprints drop from specific dungeon bosses; Blizzard does not say which ones.[4]
