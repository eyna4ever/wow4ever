Nine five-player dungeons at launch, then three raids on December 9, 2026: Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair.

In brief

World of Warcraft: Forever opens nine new five-player dungeons from level 13 to 60. On December 9, 2026 three level-60 raids unlock: Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20 players) and Onyxia’s Lair (40 players).[1][2][3]

Key points

  • Nine five-player dungeons across the Eastern Kingdoms and Kalimdor.[1]
  • Raids unlock on December 9, 2026 for level-60 characters.[2][3]
  • Onyxia’s Lair also requires the Drakefire Amulet, not only level 60.[2]
  • More raids and dungeons are announced after launch, with no date.[3]

The nine launch dungeons

Blizzard presents nine five-player dungeons “rooted in the original World of Warcraft”, with new threats, quests and adventures.[1]

For Hall of Thanes, Alliance players reach the entrance easily from their capital; Horde groups must travel into Alliance territory.[1]

The three December 9 raids

Level-60 raids[2]
RaidSizeLocation
Barrow Deeps10 playersWithin Mount Hyjal; entrances via Hyjal, Winterspring and Blackmaw Hold
Hyjal Summit20 playersSummit of Mount Hyjal near Nordrassil
Onyxia’s Lair40 playersDustwallow Marsh

Barrow Deeps is the prison that held Illidan Stormrage for 10,000 years; players descend to learn what became of those left behind.[2]

Entering Onyxia’s Lair first requires earning the Drakefire Amulet; Blizzard details that attunement in the raid overview.[2]

After December 9

The roadmap lists more raids and dungeons after launch, with no published schedule.[3]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Nine five-player dungeons at launch, with the level ranges listed above.[1]
  • Three raids on December 9: 10, 20 and 40 players.[2]
  • Drakefire Amulet required for Onyxia.[2]

Still to be confirmed

  • No date is given for later raids and dungeons, or for the “revamped iconic raid”.
  • Sources do not yet publish full boss details beyond the general overviews.

Source discrepancies

  • The beta announcement said “through October 21”; the forum confirmed October 23. That does not change the raid dates.[4][5]

Key dates

  1. 20 October 2026Dungeon and raid overviews published.[1][2]
  2. 4 November 2026Launch (dungeons available with the game).[6]
  3. 9 December 2026The three level-60 raids unlock.[2][3]
