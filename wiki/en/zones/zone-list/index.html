    In brief

    Index of zones present in the NPC list of the unofficial Forever database, consulted on 12 November 2026: 51 zones with a sheet, 2837 NPCs, plus 26 dungeons or raids already covered by an instance sheet or the bestiary (245 NPCs), and 202 NPCs with no zone given.[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Key points

    • 51 zone sheets, including the four already online (Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Mount Hyjal, Shen’dralas) revised rather than duplicated.[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • Zone level and flight points: not given. 202 NPCs with no zone: see /en/creatures/bestiary-unlocated-zone/.[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Zone not given

    202 NPCs in the list have no zone. They are already grouped in the bestiary: /en/creatures/bestiary-unlocated-zone/. No zone sheet is created for that absence.[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Correction history

    • 13 November 2026 — first draft of the index. Draft, unpublished.[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed
    • 17 November 2026 (v42): the NPC list is now marked as coming from an unofficial Forever database, to be confirmed (it was wrongly shown as “game files”).[2] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • The four zones already online (Zephras Isle, Riverglades, Mount Hyjal, Shen’dralas) are the ones Blizzard presented in the official articles cited; the other zones in this index have no official sheet in those same articles.[1]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Each zone’s level and flight points: not given. Tagged vendors are incomplete (30 tags in all). Riverglades and Mount Hyjal have no NPC in this list.
    • NPCs, vendors, trainers, positions and quest items: unofficial Forever database, to be confirmed in game.
