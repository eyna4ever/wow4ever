    Official overview (October 29, 2026)

    • A long-buried mystery between Mulgore and Desolace opens a new adventure involving the Shen’dralar, Eldre’Thalas, Dire Maul and the region’s centaur tribes.[2]
    • Wording differs between sources: “south of Desolace through the Valley of Bones” (Found Photos panel) and “between Mulgore and Desolace” (October 29). Both are kept as published.[2]
