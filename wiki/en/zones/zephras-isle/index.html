    In brief

    Skyborne starting zone announced for levels 1–12, where the protective spirits have vanished.[1]

    Key points

    • Zephras offers a level 1–12 elemental starting experience high in the sky, blending classic Warcraft style with Skywall-inspired architecture.[2]
    • At character creation, a Skyborne joins the Horde with the Windshapers or the Alliance with the High Order.[2]

    Official overview (October 29, 2026)

    • Zephras offers a level 1–12 elemental starting experience high in the sky, blending classic Warcraft style with Skywall-inspired architecture.[2]
    • At character creation, a Skyborne joins the Horde with the Windshapers or the Alliance with the High Order.[2]
