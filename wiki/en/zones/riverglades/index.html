    In brief

    Region reached by the Steamwheedle sea route through Powderfuse Port.[1]

    Key points

    • A wild land of rivers, grasslands and trade roads where struggling communities live amid old grudges; over 150 new quests covering much of the level 35–45 journey.[2]
    • The Krol’dok Stronghold dungeon is located in the Riverglades.[3]
    • 1 quests attached to this zone in the beta quest index (to be confirmed).[5] Unconfirmed — to be confirmed

    Official overview (October 29, 2026)

    • A wild land of rivers, grasslands and trade roads where struggling communities live amid old grudges; over 150 new quests covering much of the level 35–45 journey.[2]
    • The Krol’dok Stronghold dungeon is located in the Riverglades.[3]
