New and reimagined zones: Zephras, Riverglades, Hyjal and Shen’dralas

Four regions highlighted by Blizzard for the 1–60 journey, with more than a thousand new quests announced.

In brief

Forever returns players to the original continents with new or reimagined regions. Blizzard especially highlights Zephras Isle, the Riverglades, Mount Hyjal and Shen’dralas, and announces more than a thousand new quests across levels 1–60.[1][2]

Key points

  • Zephras: Skyborne 1–12 start, classic aesthetics mixed with Skywall-inspired architecture.[3][1]
  • Riverglades: mid-30s to mid-40s frontier, more than 150 quests.[1]
  • Mount Hyjal: aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat, Darkwhisper Gorge.[1]
  • Shen’dralas: mystery between Mulgore and Desolace, tied to the Shen’dralar and Dire Maul.[1]

Zephras Isle

Zephras is the level 1–12 starting experience. Its architecture blends classic Warcraft aesthetics with Skywall-inspired design, and it is home to the Skyborne.[3][1]

Riverglades

The Riverglades are described as a rugged frontier of rivers, grasslands, trade routes, struggling communities and old grudges, with more than 150 new quests across much of the mid-30s to mid-40s experience.[1]

Krol’dok Stronghold, a level 40–45 dungeon, is located there.[4]

Mount Hyjal

Mount Hyjal invites players to explore the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat, travel through Darkwhisper Gorge and help shape the future of a recovering land. Barrow Deeps and Hyjal Summit raids are tied to it.[1][5]

Shen’dralas

Shen’dralas uncovers a long-hidden mystery between Mulgore and Desolace, tied to the Shen’dralar, Eldre’Thalas / Dire Maul and the centaur tribes of the region.[1]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • More than a thousand new quests announced across levels 1–60.[1]
  • Four highlighted regions: Zephras, Riverglades, Hyjal, Shen’dralas.[1]

Still to be confirmed

  • A full list of every subzone and NPC is not published.
  • Further outdoor content updates are announced after launch, with no date.

Key dates
