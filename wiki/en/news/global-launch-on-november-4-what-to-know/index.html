    Introduction

    World of Warcraft: Forever's worldwide launch is set for November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST (23:00 UTC; midnight November 5 in Paris). Blizzard presents it as a permanent home alongside the modern game and Classic, set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core.[1]

    Key takeaways

    • Launch: November 4 at 3 p.m. PST, 23:00 UTC, midnight November 5 in Paris.[1]
    • Levels 1–60, 1,000+ new quests, new or reimagined zones: Mount Hyjal, Shen’dralas, the Riverglades, Zephras Isle.[1]
    • New race: the Skyborne (Windshapers for the Horde, High Order for the Alliance).[1]
    • Nine new five-player dungeons (levels 13–60); three raids on December 9.[1]
    • Hardcore Ruleset: "sometime after launch", no date.[1]

    Launch night: two official clarifications

    • Time: on November 4, Blizzard's French customer support confirmed that WoW Forever was launching that night at midnight Paris time, not at 2 p.m. on November 5, replying to a player who had spotted that error in a launch email.[2]
    • Error WOW51900337: the same day, Blizzard explained that this login error before launch was expected, "as the game is not live yet".[3][4]

    First login

    On first login the game asks for an Experience Preset, Classic or Enhanced, before the Ruleset. Blizzard suggests Enhanced if unsure; every setting can be changed in Options, and the screen appears only once. There is no realm list: you pick a Ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplay, Roleplay PvP). Each character has a two-part name forming a full name unique within the region. HD or SD models are chosen at each character creation and can be changed later (Options or barber).[1]

    Systems and content

    The announcement recalls the Campfire (up to three camp items crafted by nearby players, a bonus after at least one minute sitting or crafting by the fire) and the Legacy system, an account-wide horizontal progression. The Seasons Depths, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia's Lair raids open on December 9 for level 60 characters. The Darkspear Islands battleground (15 vs 15) is part of PvP.[1]

    First days: hotfixes and known issues

    From November 5, Blizzard posted several official forum messages about Forever's first days.[5][8]

    • Hotfix live on November 5 (02:43 UTC): players who joined with an Invite-a-Friend code are no longer restricted (chat channels, levelling past 10, guilds, groups, trading). Log out and back in if needed.[5]
    • Boats and zeppelins could get lost "in the maelstrom": Blizzard added temporary portals near docks and zeppelin towers. A permanent fix was announced for the morning of November 5 (US time), then portal removal once verified. As of November 6, no later blue post confirms that permanent fix is live or that the portals were removed — announced, not confirmed shipped.[6]
    • Some areas feel underpopulated: Blizzard said it would tweak settings. As of November 6, no blue post confirms that tweak was applied — announced, not confirmed shipped.[7]
    • Official tip: check in-game language and social settings, change them if needed and log back in.[9]

    Official known issues (thread "Known Issues - WoW Forever - Updated Nov. 5", last edit checked: November 5, 2026 at 22:07 UTC).[8]

    • Dungeons: cauldron near Roogug in Razorfen Kraul that players can fall into and get stuck.[8]
    • Gamepad: error when deleting more than one rare or epic item; push-to-talk keyboard button instructions may show when joining a voice channel.[8]
    • NPCs: PvP vendors temporarily unavailable (Orgrimmar, Stormwind, Dalaran); Stormwind guards incorrectly direct players to Fyrenz Stagazer for Transmogrification — correct NPC name is Fyrenz Vishonar.[8]
    • Quests: "Vorsha the Lasher" in Ashenvale disabled until a crash is fixed.[8]
    • UI: bank pages displaying in pages, tabs listed outside the frame.[8]
    • World: Stormwind Keep entrance may not look as intended, including unintended flashing textures.[8]

    What’s next?

    Key dates

    2. 9 December 2026The three raids open to level 60 characters.[1]

    Confirmed by official sources

    • No realm list: each character is created in a Ruleset (Normal, PvP, Roleplay, Roleplay PvP).[1]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Hardcore Ruleset date: not announced ("sometime after launch").
    • Official French names for Razorfen Kraul / Roogug, "Vorsha the Lasher", Stormwind Keep, Fyrenz Stagazer / Fyrenz Vishonar (Known Issues thread is English-only).
    • Confirmation that the permanent transport fix and the population tweak have actually shipped.
