The World of Warcraft: Forever beta (September 17 – October 23, 2026)

Access, tested content, stress test and closure: what Blizzard published about the beta, which has now ended.

In brief

The World of Warcraft: Forever beta opened on September 17, 2026 and closed on October 23, 2026, about five weeks in all. This recap gathers what Blizzard said about it: who could play, what was tested and how it ended.[1][2]

Key points

  • Access: selected opted-in accounts, plus purchasers of the Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection.[1][3]
  • At the start: level cap 20 (raised to 30 later), with the Skyborne, Zephras Isle and two dungeons.[1]
  • Closure on October 23 at 10:00 a.m. PDT (19:00 Paris) after a final in-game celebration day.[4][2]

Who could take part

Opted-in players could be selected, with invitations sent regularly; eligible digital bundle purchasers also had access. As a guard against phishing, Blizzard advised logging in to the Battle.net account to verify any invitation in doubt.[1]

The Skyborne Epic Pack and the limited-time Warcraft Forever Collection included beta access.[3][1]

What was tested

The beta began with a level cap of 20, later raised to 30. Week 1 offered the Skyborne and Zephras Isle, the Hall of Thanes (13–18) and Ruins of Lordaeron (15–20) dungeons, and all zone content up to level 20.[1]

The class deep dives published during the beta warned that abilities and talents could still change.[5][6]

The October 21 stress test

The stress test was open to everyone with no subscription, Game Time, invitation or beta access needed. It moved from October 19 to October 21, and characters, names and guilds created there were to be wiped before name reservations opened on October 27.[7]

The closure

On October 22 the official forum announced the beta would end the next day, Friday October 23 at 10:00 a.m. PDT, with 24 hours of festivities in Zephras Isle, Dun Morogh, Elwynn Forest, Teldrassil, Durotar, Tirisfal Glades and Mulgore; Blizzard warned that items and experiences from this one-time celebration would almost certainly not be part of the launched game.[4]

On October 23, community manager Kaivax confirmed: “The WoW Forever Beta is now closed”, announcing a few days of work on priority issues and the game service before the November 4 launch.[2]

Episode 5 of the official podcast, an end-of-beta special, features host Countdown To Classic, guest Meeix and four team members: Josh “Aggrend” Greenfield, Clay Stone, Ana Resendez and Michael Nuthals.[8][9]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Beta opened on September 17, 2026.[1]
  • Beta closed on October 23, 2026.[2]
  • Launch still set for November 4, 2026.[2]

Still to be confirmed

  • The end-of-beta podcast is only available as video with no official transcript, so it is not summarized here.
  • The sources consulted do not say when the level cap was raised to 30, nor which later zones and dungeons were opened.
  • The outcomes of the beta (fixes, tuning) are not detailed.

Source discrepancies

  • The beta announcement called October 21 the last full day of testing; the actual closure, confirmed on the forum, was October 23.[1][2]

Key dates

  2. 21 October 2026Stress test open to all.[7]
  3. 22 October 2026End-of-beta celebration day and special podcast.[4][8]
  4. 23 October 2026Beta closes (19:00 Paris).[2]
