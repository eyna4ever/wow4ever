    Forever: Blizzard removes gold and suspends accounts after the Blackmaw Hold loot abuse

    On November 17, Blizzard explained on the official World of Warcraft: Forever forums how it is handling the abuse of a loot bug in the Blackmaw Hold dungeon: removal of gold earned through the exploit and account suspensions for the most serious cases.
