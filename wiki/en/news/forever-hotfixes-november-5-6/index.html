    Introduction

    Blizzard keeps a cumulative Forever hotfix page, now titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 23, 2026”. It covers the November 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 11, 13, 16, 17, 19, 20 and 23 batches. The French page of article 24315281 still carries an older title (“November 13” as of November 20), so spell, NPC, item and quest names are kept in English. On November 12 at 00:48 UTC, Kaivax postponed the November 10 Auction House changes to November 17. On November 13, dungeon enemies grant no XP to the whole party once they are “grey” to at least one member; training lost on November 9 is refunded in gold. November 10 class tuning remains in the dedicated article.[1][2][10][11][14][17][24]

    Key takeaways

    • November 23: world buffs no longer fall off when changing areas; Darkmoon Faire and Fengus’ Ferocity buffs are halved; Mortal Strike can no longer be used without the talent; fixes for Uldaman, foods, engineering and quests.[24]
    • Known issues in progress: a temporary lockbox behaviour change on November 22, with a proper fix in the works; the epic riding skill is not granted after Paladin and Warlock mount quest chains, with a fix expected “in another day or two”.[27][29]
    • November 20: players can now reset and enter each dungeon at most 5 times per hour (was 10); Stormshroud Armor bonus lowered, Demonic Aegis bug fixed, Summit of Eternity forge usable again.[17]
    • November 19: the Seed Hybridizer is re-enabled, with adjusted values and a chance to fail; fixes for Retribution Paladin, dungeons, quests, the Skyborne riding discount and Tauren Cultivation.[17]
    • November 17: Upper Blackrock Spire enemy health and damage are raised back; the Paladin tanks’ Soulforge Armor now gives Spell Power instead of Strength; PvP, profession and quest fixes.[17]
    • November 16: class (including Windfury Totem), dungeon, item, PvP and quest fixes; with weekly maintenance, Vampiric Embrace healing drops from 20% to 10% of the priest’s Shadow damage, with no change to solo play according to Blizzard.[14]
    • November 14: dungeon XP is nullified only when a member is more than 12 levels above the enemies.[12]
    • On November 13, dungeon enemies stop giving any XP to the whole party as soon as they are trivial (“grey”) to at least one member; class, item and quest fixes also landed. Training bought and lost during the November 9 server incident (22:17–23:17 CET) is refunded in gold by in-game mail.[1][10][11]
    • November 11: class fixes (detailed in the class-tuning article), Item Restoration disabled while a critical bug is investigated, reduced damage in Riverglades, larger tailoring bags, several quests, and mounting in Shen'dralas.[1]
    • November 10: most quests level 48 and above now also reward gold; profession adjustments; shaman totems lost in the November 9 crash restored. Auction House changes from this batch were temporarily removed and postponed to November 17.[1][2][3][7]
    • On November 9 the official page lists a 50% increase to dungeon kill XP; Kaivax says the November 8 hotfix took that XP from 40% to 60% of Classic Era values, which the team currently considers satisfactory.[1][5][8]

    November 23, 2026

    Batch published on the official page, now titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 23, 2026”, and relayed by Kaivax on the official US and EU forums on November 24 at 03:06 Paris time. Spell, NPC, item and quest names are kept in English.[24][25][26]

    • Classes: Druid: the 4-piece Feralheart set now correctly grants 4 Energy every second for 5 seconds. Priest: Mind Vision no longer procs effects; Shadow: Dark Inspiration grants the correct Spirit bonus when activated by Mind Flay, and Chastise is usable in Shadowform. Destruction Warlock: Intensity now works on Incinerate. Warrior: Mortal Strike stays in the spellbook but can no longer be used without the talent point.[24]
    • Dungeons: Uldaman, the Obsidian Sentinel drops loot as intended.[24]
    • Items: the Filcher’s Cowl no longer has a level requirement.[24]
    • Professions: Cooking, clam dishes (Boiled Clams, Clam Chowder, Goblin Deviled Clams, Giant Clam Scorcho, Undermine Clam Chowder, Clam Linguine) give the correct Well Fed bonus and are listed under “Stamina Food”; Murloc Fin Soup moves up to the next food tier. Engineering: Goblin and Gnome Engineer’s Renewal Gift no longer show as unusable question marks, and “The Mortar: Reloaded” is learned with “Goblin Mortar”. Leatherworking: Pattern: Centaur Hoofwarmers is learnable again.[24]
    • Quests: Riverglades, Dunston Willis runs again near the end of the escort (“Dunston Checks Out”); Wetlands, “For Further Study” now has rewards and “Uncovering the Past” / “Gleaning Our Future” reward Ironforge reputation.[24]
    • Races: Wisp Form can no longer be applied to living players.[24]
    • Spells: a few spells caused more weapon procs than intended (fixed); World Buffs no longer fall off when changing areas (they still fall off on entering raids); Fengus’ Ferocity and Darkmoon Faire buffs granted twice the intended power (fixed).[24]
    • What it means for players: world buffs persist across zones; untalented warriors can no longer use Mortal Strike; Darkmoon and Fengus buffs are halved; affected Paladins and Warlocks wait for the riding fix.[24][29]

    Known issues in progress

    • Lockboxes received a temporary behaviour change on November 22 at 9:55 a.m. PST; Blizzard is working on a proper fix that restores peeking and partial looting, with no timeline yet.[27][28]
    • The epic riding skill is not granted after class epic mount quest chains; Blizzard believes Paladins and Warlocks are affected and expects a fix “in another day or two”.[29][30]

    November 20, 2026

    Batch published on the official page, now titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 20, 2026”, and relayed by Kaivax on the official US and EU forums on November 21 at 01:18 Paris time.[17][22][23]

    • Classes: Combat Rogue: Blade Flurry now correctly makes Shank’s damage hit an additional target.[17]
    • Classes: Demonology Warlock: fixed a bug that let Demonic Aegis effects be snapshotted into Demon Armor or Demon Skin and kept after untalenting; Demonic Aegis and Demon Skin are removed when talents are unlearned.[17]
    • Dungeons: players can now reset and enter each dungeon at most 5 times per hour (was 10). Blackrock Spire: a stone pillar no longer traps adventurers.[17]
    • Enemies and NPCs: Orgrimmar, Mayhoa Skyhoof no longer has glowing eyes.[17]
    • Items: Stormshroud Armor 3-piece bonus lowered to a 3% chance (was 4%) on melee attack to restore 18 energy (was 30).[17]
    • Professions: Toron Rockhoof’s forge and anvil in Summit of Eternity are usable again for blacksmithing and smelting.[17]
    • Quests: The Hinterlands, Witch Doctor Ti’ik drops multiple copies of his head for a group on the quest; Feralas, Perfect Yeti Hide no longer drops while its quest is active; Tanaris, Gor’marok respawns faster (“The Dunemaul Compound”); Riverglades, Dunston Willis no longer runs off during “Dunston Checks Out”.[17]
    • User Interface: fixed an issue that showed fewer groups than intended in group finder.[17]
    • What it means for players: repeated dungeon farming is slowed (5 entries per hour); Stormshroud Armor wearers lose energy; the Demonic Aegis trick no longer works.[17]

    November 19, 2026

    Batch published on the official page, then titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 19, 2026”, and relayed by Kaivax on the official US and EU forums on November 20 at 05:00 Paris time.[17][20][21]

    • Camping: the Seed Hybridizer has been re-enabled; Seed Hybridization has adjusted values and can now fail; Scarce Herb Seeds can no longer be used in it.[17]
    • Classes: Retribution Paladin: Two-Handed Weapon Specialization now correctly increases all Holy damage dealt while a two-handed weapon is equipped.[17]
    • Dungeons: City of Dalaran: Shade of the Archmage no longer despawns on entering combat; The Shaper’s Terrace: Vitrix’s Poison Shock deals less damage.[17]
    • Enemies: Shimmering Flats, a Silithid Hive Drone stuck under the world near Rustmaul Dig Site is fixed; Riverglades, Mosh’gra can drop rare loot again.[17]
    • Items: Motes of Unstable Magic are now soulbound; Squall-breaker’s Potion can now be deleted.[17]
    • Quests: fixes in Booty Bay (Rix Xizzix after “Rift Away”), Alterac Mountains (new Ensorcelled Parchment for “Stormpike’s Deciphering”) and for “Hunting for Ectoplasm” (correct creature type).[17]
    • Races: Skyborne now correctly receive the Honored 10% discount on Apprentice and Journeyman Riding sold by Genn Fairweather on Zephras Isle; Tauren: Cultivation succeeds more reliably on herbs you have the skill for and works on Purple Lotus.[17]
    • What it means for players: seed hybridization is back but riskier; Honored Skyborne finally pay less for riding; Motes of Unstable Magic can no longer be traded.[17]

    November 17, 2026

    Batch published on the official page, then titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 17, 2026”, and relayed by Kaivax on the official US and EU forums on November 18 at 04:24 Paris time.[17][18][19]

    • Camping: mounted players can no longer sleep on the Cozy Sleeping Bag; it can be placed in specific spots such as hunter traps and shows a Resting aura within its area of effect.[17]
    • Classes: Might of Stormwind and Warchief’s Blessing now correctly cancel on death and no longer apply to non-players; Shaman: Flurry no longer wrongly loses stacks when Windfury totem attacks land.[17]
    • Dungeons: enemy health and damage in Upper Blackrock Spire were significantly too low; this is fixed, and Blizzard says the dungeon again needs a full group of diligent players.[17]
    • Enemies: fixes for creatures stuck in rocks in Thousand Needles (Pesterhide Snarler, Needles Cougar, Gravelsnout Digger/Surveyor).[17]
    • Items: Shadow priests can hold Night Watchman’s Torch; Escape Plan has correct range and damage; Whiteout Staff and Crackling Staff now share the same stats (25 Stamina, 25 Intellect, 74 Spell Power). Paladin: Soulforge Armor (upgraded tank dungeon set) now has Spell Power instead of Strength, as Blizzard leans into Paladin tanks favoring Spell Power.[17]
    • Legacy: the Reagent Economy should no longer randomly stop working.[17]
    • PvP: no more mounting in the Warsong Gulch starting rooms; the Battle for Alterac Valley quests require level 58.[17]
    • Professions: fix for some Waylaid Crate conversions; Blacksmithing: “The Old Ways” grants the Orcish War Leggings recipe via an item (replacement from Sumi), Bronze Mace is now bind-on-equip; Herbalism: Cultivation works on Mageroyal; Skinning: a few normally unskinnable creatures can no longer be skinned.[17]
    • Quests: fixes for “The Manor, Ravenholdt” (Mathias Shaw’s letter), “Blisters on the Land”, “Step One: Rock”, “A New Ore Sample”, “Agamaggan’s Blood” (no longer a dungeon quest) and several Shen’dralas quests (“Powering the Pylons”, “Heart of Crystal”, “Going Home”); Spectral Iron Ore spawns more and can be tracked.[17]
    • UI: ignore lists now persist after logging out.[17]
    • What it means for players: groups used to clearing Upper Blackrock Spire easily should expect a much harder dungeon; Paladin tanks wearing Soulforge Armor gain Spell Power.[17]

    November 16, 2026

    Batch published on the official page, now titled “WoW: Forever Hotfixes - November 16, 2026”, and relayed by Kaivax on the official US and EU forums on November 17 at 03:17 Paris time.[14][15][16]

    • Camping: the Fishing Rack now gives lures to everyone; Bloated Fish caught through it drop more often and have adjusted loot.[14]
    • Druid: Wild Growth ranks now correctly go on cooldown; Balance: Genesis now increases Pounce’s bleed damage.[14]
    • Priest: Dark Inspiration no longer stacks with several Shadow priests in the party. With weekly maintenance, Vampiric Embrace healing drops from 20% to 10% of the priest’s Shadow damage, and the priest receives 100% more healing from the effect; Blizzard says solo play is unchanged.[14]
    • Rogue: poison ranks can no longer exceed the maximum rank; Shank no longer wrongly removes combo points held on other targets.[14]
    • Shaman: several Windfury Totem fixes (ignored armor and no internal cooldown, no Rage generated, extra procs); Enhancement: Maelstrom Weapon no longer benefits two casts, Thundering Strikes no longer needs a weapon equipped.[14]
    • Warrior: the Revered Champion offers Legacy of Valor to eligible warriors again; Fury: Cleave and Heroic Strike now trigger Unbridled Wrath and Reckless Swings and no longer consume Flurry.[14]
    • Dungeons: in The Drowned City, Captain Dreadrise is no longer stun-immune, his Abyssal Bubble can no longer block/dodge/parry and the rare “Pyromancer” Braxton now reacts to combat; Sunken Temple: fix for Atal’alarion serpent statues activated in the wrong order.[14]
    • Items: Formula: Enchant Boots - Speed (reputation) is now bind-on-pickup; Outrider’s Gloves work; the Violet Tragan no longer breaks crowd control.[14]
    • PvP: dying in the Arathi Basin Gold Mine no longer sends you to a Riverglades graveyard.[14]
    • Quests: “Blood Red Bandanas” drop more often; fixes for “The Talisman of Kazdor”, “No Soldier Left Behind” (credit for the whole party), “Screecher Spirits” (Vale Screamers moved to the Isle of Dread coast) and “The Return of the King?” (Theramore); “Water Pouch Bounty” no longer grants XP.[14]
    • Consequences for players: Shadow priests in groups heal allies less through Vampiric Embrace but heal themselves more; shaman/warrior groups lose the Windfury attacks that ignored armor.[14]

    November 14: dungeon XP rule relaxed

    Kaivax (official US and EU forums, posted November 15 at 02:18 Paris time): the November 13 rule “missed the mark” in some dungeons because of the enemies’ level range. With hotfixes now live, XP nullification only applies when the high-level player is more than 12 levels above the enemies. Instances already underway when it was deployed are not affected.[12][13]

    November 13, 2026

    Killing dungeon enemies now awards zero experience to all party members if the enemy is trivial to one or more of them. Non-boss enemies in The Drowned City respawn after 2 hours (was 1); Sneed (The Deadmines) ejects properly; Balnazzar (Stratholme) counts toward the Master Spelunker legacy challenge.[1][10]

    Classes: diminishing returns restored for stuns between players and creatures. Restoration Druid: Wild Growth can no longer be cast while moving, healing coefficient raised to 6.6% (was 3.3%). Paladin: Judgement of Command triggers on-hit effects and counts as a melee attack (other judgements stay ranged). Shaman: Windfury Totem procs from all melee abilities except off-hand strikes. Fixes also for Mage (Frost Nova), Priest (The Quick and the Dead perk), Warlock (Siphon Life Rank 1) and Warrior (Berserker Rage vs Sap).[1]

    Items: stats corrected on about ten items; Channeler's Ring now has spell power (was Spirit) and Blessed Band of Light loses its hit. Blessed Band of Light owners may swap it, irreversibly, for a Channeler's Ring at Pix Xizzix until raids open.[1]

    Quests and world: around fifteen quest fixes (party-shared quest items, Riverglades, Feralas, Mount Hyjal); spawn tweaks (quieter Mulgore start, busier Stranglethorn and Badlands); many more winners per ruleset in the weekly Stranglethorn fishing tournament. User interface: action bars no longer reset on logout for characters with Secondary Talents.[1]

    Refund: training bought and lost during the November 9 server incident (22:17–23:17 CET) is refunded in gold by in-game mail (announced November 14, 01:36 UTC).[11]

    Player impact: grouping in a dungeon with a much higher-level character can now cost the whole party XP on enemies grey to that character. Blizzard says it keeps working on fishing tournament performance, with more fixes in the coming weeks.[1]

    November 11, 2026

    The November 11 batch is on the official page updated on November 12 at 00:46 UTC, and in a Kaivax post at 00:48 UTC. That day’s class hotfixes (Feral Druid, Hunter, Paladin, Priest and Shaman) are presented in the November 10 class-tuning article, so the list appears only once.[1][2]

    The Item Restoration service has been disabled while Blizzard investigates and addresses a critical bug. In Riverglades, damage from the Twilight Enforcer’s Smash was significantly reduced, Executioner Morg’s damage was lowered, and the Twilight Forgemaster’s Mark of Flames now increases damage dealt by 50 (was 1,000). Bloody Claws of Attack correctly add +1 Fist/Unarmed Weapon skill; Earthen Silk Slippers no longer give more spell damage than intended.[1]

    In Tailoring, several bags that were unintentionally smaller than intended had their slot size and materials adjusted: Red Mageweave Bag is now 14 slots and requires 5 bolts of mageweave (was 4); Green Silk Pack and Black Silk Pack are now 12 slots (5 and 6 bolts of silk, was 4 and 5); Green Woolen Bag and Red Woolen Bag are now 10 slots and require 5 bolts of wool (was 4).[1]

    Quests: players can again interact with the Command Tent on “Scarlet Diversions” (Western Plaguelands); the Witherbark Mallet works again for “The Giant in the Den” (Arathi Highlands); the Cage Door for “Freedom for All Creatures” (Feralas) resets again. A bug that prevented mounting in named areas of Shen'dralas has been fixed.[1]

    November 10, 2026

    Most quests level 48 and above now also reward gold, including some that never did. Quests that already give valuable items, repeatable quests and very short quests are excluded. Forever does not have the XP-to-gold conversion for quests turned in at level 60, added to the original game shortly before The Burning Crusade: Blizzard says it does not want players to hoard quests until 60, and acknowledges that only about 25% of original quests gave gold, so income dropped more than intended.[1][3]

    Auction House changes that were part of this batch (minimum deposit lowered to 1 copper from 1 silver, mail wrongly shown as from the Stormwind Auction House, and some mail arriving in 2 minutes instead of 60) have been temporarily removed. On November 12 at 00:48 UTC, Kaivax said they will go live with scheduled weekly maintenance on November 17 and will appear again in the hotfixes update thereafter. They are no longer on the official page updated at 00:46 UTC the same day.[2][1]

    All racial vendor mounts are now Bind-on-Pickup. The vendor price was lowered for some bound crafted armor from Leatherworking, Tailoring and Blacksmithing: Blizzard says these were meant to be crafted only once for the player. Changes to Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering and Fishing; Swiftstrike Shot and Swiftfeather Arrow are no longer Bind-on-Pickup (only available once raids unlock). Blizzard acknowledges other open Fishing Rack issues, beyond a fix already applied to some “There is no loot” errors.[1]

    About ten quests were fixed, including the “Call of Water” chain, with more creatures for several objectives and graveyard corrections. Shatterspear Trolls no longer grant XP. Shamans who lost a totem in the November 9 server crash should find it restored to their bags: Kaivax asks them to make bag space and relog.[1][7]

    November 9, 2026

    The official page includes in this batch the 50% increase to dungeon kill experience, already deployed on November 8. On November 10 Kaivax said that hotfix took dungeon kill XP from 40% to 60% of Classic Era values, and that the team currently considers the result satisfactory (no further increase was announced).[1][5][8]

    Many class fixes: Druid (Feral Charge, Natural Instinct, Pounce), Hunter (pet families, long-range aggro when targeting), Mage (Arcane Concentration, Flamestrike ground ticks and Clearcasting), Paladin (Consecration), Priest (Shadowform with Fear Ward, Power Infusion on allies), Shaman (Lightning Overload damage), Warlock and Warrior. Completing “Summon Felsteed” correctly grants the riding skill; players who finished it earlier get it by summoning the Felsteed once. Incinerate is no longer available outside Destruction.[1]

    In The Drowned City, creatures dealt about 30% more damage than intended. In Deathless Marrow, creature health is down 25% and several abilities were weakened. Loot fixes (Alcaz Prison, Shaper's Terrace) and blocker fixes (Blackfathom Deeps, Blackrock Depths). Mounting inside Stratholme is no longer possible. The Pouch of Violet Dust now grants the extra hour of Camp Benefits; Waylaid Crates and Craftsman's Writs drop in dungeons again. An invisible wall was removed from the Alliance base roof in Warsong Gulch. Player population was lowered in several congested zones, with a bigger change on the PvP ruleset; Blizzard says it will keep monitoring and adjusting.[1]

    November 8, 2026: dungeon enemy XP

    At about 20:05 Paris time (11:05 PST), Blizzard deployed a hotfix that increased dungeon kill experience by 50%. That deployment was first announced on the official forums, then listed on the cumulative page in the November 9 batch. On November 10, Kaivax said dungeon kill XP went from 40% to 60% of Classic Era values, a setting the team currently considers satisfactory.[8][9][1][5]

    The official November 8 post did not mention any change to quest XP or dungeon completion rewards. The extra gold for most quests level 48 and above belongs to the November 10 batch, described above.[8][9][1]

    Classes: targeted fixes

    The November 6 batch fixes Bloom so it heals its target, plus Druid tooltips for Bear Form, Ravage and Swiftmend. Blizzard explicitly says Ravage damage did not change; only the displayed value was wrong.[1]

    Voidwalker base health increases by about 40 at all levels. Soul Siphon no longer allows Wrack to increase its own damage. Mages should have Study rather than Research after the relevant quest; Arcane Blast’s duration is corrected to 18 seconds.[1]

    Dungeons, quests and professions

    Zaetar’s Spirit should appear after Princess Theradras in Maraudon. Erroneous creature disengagements in Blackmaw Hold and The Drowned City are fixed, as is The Drowned City’s instance portal.[1]

    For professions, Tanning Rack now requires 125 skill rather than 140; its skill-up thresholds are lowered by 15. Quest fixes include shared quest-item drops for parties in Riverglades, a supplied Horn of Awakening to wake Kerlonian, and Horde access to “Unending Torment” at level 15 instead of 16.[1]

    The November 5 batch and player impact

    Plainsrunning can no longer stack, including during battleground preparation; stacks are cancelled while carrying a flag. Zephras tradeskill supply vendors sell the missing apprentice bags. Mild Spices now sells to vendors for 1 copper rather than 2; this is not a reduction in its purchase price.[1]

    NPC fixes include the respawn timing of Yarrog Baneshadow and some murlocs, not their movement speed. This selection does not replace the full official list. English names are retained where their Forever translations are not confirmed.[1]

    WoW4ever AI editorial analysis

    Opinion · AIWoW4ever AI editorial analysis

    The useful distinction remains between functionality fixes, tooltip corrections and power changes: a corrected tooltip is not necessarily a buff. The November 11 batch mixes quest and Riverglades damage fixes, a concrete change to bag size, and the temporary disabling of Item Restoration, which can block item recovery while the bug is investigated. The Auction House postponement to November 17 shows that measures already listed can be withdrawn afterwards: the official page and Kaivax’s November 12 post are the record, not an earlier list. November 10 class tuning, now with official numbers, is covered in the dedicated article The November 13 dungeon XP rule clearly targets high-level “boosting”; it also affects friends with wide level gaps, which explains the strong forum reactions. Whether Blizzard softens the threshold remains to be seen. This batch mostly cleans up talent and quest bugs; the only real balance change targets the Shadow priest in groups, leaving solo play untouched. Restoring Upper Blackrock Spire’s difficulty is the most visible change in the November 17 batch; the rest confirms a deliberate direction for Paladin tanks, built around Spell Power. The November 19 batch is lighter; bringing back the Seed Hybridizer with a failure chance suggests a wish to curb economic abuse rather than remove the feature. Halving the reset limit on November 20 clearly targets intensive instance farming; it is one more economic lever after the Blackmaw Hold affair, at some cost in flexibility for groups that restart often. Keeping world buffs across zones while halving the Darkmoon and Fengus buffs rebalances their weight rather than removing them; it can be read as a wish to keep these bonuses attractive without making them mandatory.
