    November 10 class tuning: Enhancement Shaman, Affliction Warlock and pets

    Introduction

    On November 6, on the official forums, Blizzard announced a set of class adjustments for Tuesday, November 10, aimed at tank pets, Affliction Warlock and Enhancement Shaman. The numbers now appear in the official November 10 hotfix list. Because of a deployment delay, the Shaman changes only went live in all regions on November 12 at 10:45 a.m. PST (19:45 CET). The French page of article 24315281 returns 404, so spell names are kept in English. A separate November 11 batch adds further class hotfixes.[3][1][5][6]

    Key takeaways

    • The changes announced for November 10 are applied and listed in the official November 10 hotfix notes. Because of a deployment delay, the Shaman changes (weapon imbues stacking with weapon totems, Stormstrike, Mental Dexterity, Maelstrom Weapon and its tooltip) only went live in all regions on November 12 at 10:45 a.m. PST (19:45 CET).[3][1][5][6]
    • Enhancement Shaman: Stormstrike 100% weapon damage (was 150%) on an 8 s cooldown (was 6 s); Mental Dexterity 33/66/100% (was 50/100/150%); Maelstrom Weapon 20% per stack at every rank, 6 to 10 procs per minute by rank (was 20); the Maelstrom Weapon tooltip now shows the proc chance based on main-hand weapon speed (official example: 5 points with a 3.6-speed weapon shows 60% per swing, versus 33% with a 2.0-speed weapon).[3][5]
    • Affliction: Wrack deals 30% less damage and speeds up Siphon Life, Corruption and Bane of Agony while channeled; Soul Siphon now counts only those three spells (up to 36%). Pets: several demon base health up by about 15%; Hunter pets corrected to 115% (a bug had given them 150%).[3]

    What was applied on November 10

    These figures come from the official November 10 hotfix list. For Enhancement Shaman, Stormstrike now deals 100% weapon damage (was 150%) on an 8-second cooldown (was 6); Mental Dexterity converts Intellect to attack power at 33/66/100% (was 50/100/150%); Maelstrom Weapon grants 20% reduced cast time and mana cost per application at all ranks (was 4/8/12/16/20%) and activates 6, 7, 8, 9 or 10 times per minute depending on rank (was 20 regardless of rank). The tooltip also shows the chance to activate based on main-hand weapon speed (official example: 5 points with a 3.6-speed weapon shows 60% per swing, versus 33% with a 2.0-speed weapon).[3][5]

    Because of a deployment delay, the Shaman changes (weapon imbues stacking with weapon totems, Stormstrike, Mental Dexterity, Maelstrom Weapon and its tooltip) only went live in all regions on November 12 at 10:45 a.m. PST (19:45 CET), not with the rest of the November 10 batch. The other parts of this tuning (Warlock, pets, and the classes not covered by this post) stay dated November 10. The official hotfix page still lists these lines under November 10; the forum post fixes the actual go-live date for the Shaman changes.[5][6][3]

    Blizzard says beta changes, alongside Elemental adjustments, pushed Enhancement toward Intellect and spell-power gear and away from Windfury, which the team wants to keep at the heart of the specialization. It is acting before the raid opens, so contested items do not lose value afterwards. Weapon imbues and weapon totems can now be used together, for example Windfury Weapon with Windfury Totem, for all Shaman specializations.[3]

    For Warlocks, Voidwalker, Incubus, Succubus and Imp base health is up by about 15%, and the Imp’s first four Firebolt ranks deal more damage. For Affliction, Wrack deals 30% less damage and no longer boosts periodic damage: while it is channeled, Siphon Life, Corruption and Bane of Agony tick twice as often, and the time skipped is kept after the channel ends. Soul Siphon now counts only those three spells (4/8/12% each, up to 36%).[3]

    A bug gave Hunter pets 150% of their normal health; it is now 115%. Arcane Shot and Summon Hawk cost 11% of base mana at every rank; Hawk melee damage is up by about 80%. Nature's Swiftness also makes Wild Growth instant (Restoration Druid). Sweeping Strikes no longer loses charges without a second target (Arms Warrior). Redoubt and Maelstrom Weapon can be tracked in the Cooldown Manager.[3]

    November 11 class hotfixes

    The official page updated on November 12 at 00:46 UTC adds a November 11 class batch, separate from the numbered November 10 tuning. For Feral Druids, Rend and Tear now works on Pounce Bleed. For Hunters, Volley no longer wrongly activates procs and no longer keeps channeling after the Hunter has started moving.[3]

    Judgement is now considered a Ranged Attack: it no longer counts as a melee attack for item and effect procs, and uses the Paladin’s ranged hit chance. For Retribution, Unsealed Fury no longer generates echoes from Windfury Totem attacks (following a fix to Windfury Totem activating from Holy Strike). Confounding Flash (Gnome) and the Holy talent Twilight Focus now work correctly in Shadowform. Flametongue Totem Rank 2 is working again.[3]

    What had been announced on November 6

    For Hunter pets and Voidwalkers, Blizzard says it wants their effectiveness to be roughly the same as in Classic Era and will adjust their stats as needed.[1][2]

    For Affliction, reports show that always channeling Wrack beats also casting Siphon Life, Corruption and Bane of Agony. The stated goal is for those spells to always be worth casting alongside Wrack, without changing the spec's total damage.[1][2]

    For Enhancement, Blizzard wants Strength to always be the strongest stat so the spec no longer prefers gear meant for Mages and Warlocks. Its best items should ideally carry Strength or Attack Power, with some Intellect or Spell Power.[1][2]

    What it means for players

    Blizzard advises Enhancement Shamans who are levelling to collect Strength and Attack Power gear from now on.[1][2]

    The team says new issues may come up over the weekend and plans class balance changes on Tuesday, then on later Tuesdays if needed.[1][2]

For Enhancement, the change is broader than a simple stat tweak: Blizzard says it is partly reversing beta choices, which may lead some players to rethink their gear before the raid opens. The November 11 hotfixes are of a different kind (bug fixes and treating Judgement as a ranged attack) and do not replace that tuning pass The delay explains why some players may have seen no change in game for two days; Enhancement impressions posted before the evening of November 12 describe the old version.
