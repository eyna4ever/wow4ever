World of Warcraft: Forever schedule, from beta to the first raids

Every official date: beta, stress test, name reservation, the November 4 launch (midnight November 5 in Paris) and the December 9 raids.

In brief

Announced at BlizzCon 2026, World of Warcraft: Forever ran a beta from September 17 to October 23, 2026. It launches on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. Pacific time, which is midnight on November 5 in Paris. The first three raids open on December 9.[1][2][3][4]

Key points

  • Worldwide launch: November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST, i.e. midnight on November 5, Paris time.[1][2]
  • Early Name Reservation from October 27 to November 3 for eligible upgrades; in Europe it opens on October 27 at 18:00 CET.[5]
  • Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit and Onyxia’s Lair raids: opening on December 9.[4]
  • Hardcore and a revamped iconic raid are planned after launch, with no announced date.[2][6]

Before launch

The beta began on September 17, 2026. It was open to selected accounts and to purchasers of certain digital bundles.[7][1]

A stress test open to everyone, with no subscription or invitation required, was moved from October 19 to October 21. In Europe it started at 19:00 CEST.[8]

The beta announcement named October 21 as the “last full day of testing”. The official forum then set the end for Friday, October 23 at 10:00 a.m. PDT (19:00 in Paris), and the closure was confirmed that day.[7][9][3]

Names and character creation

Early Name Reservation runs from October 27 to November 3, 2026 for purchasers of the Skyborne Heroic Pack, the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Warcraft Forever Collection.[5][10]

According to the official forum, character creation opens to everyone on Tuesday, October 27, leaving time to pick a ruleset and a name before launch.[3]

After launch

The Barrow Deeps (10 players), Hyjal Summit (20 players) and Onyxia’s Lair (40 players) raids unlock on December 9 for level 60 characters; Onyxia’s Lair also requires earning the Drakefire Amulet.[4]

The roadmap then lists more raids and dungeons, PvP updates, world content, additional quests across levels 1–60, Hardcore and a revamped iconic raid.[2]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Launch on November 4, 2026 at 3:00 p.m. PST, midnight on November 5 in Paris.[1][2]
  • Beta closed on October 23, 2026.[3]
  • Raids open on December 9.[4]

Still to be confirmed

  • No date has been announced for Hardcore, the revamped iconic raid or later content updates.
  • The “Server Slam” mentioned in the beta announcement has no date in the sources consulted; the October 21 stress test may be its final form, but no source says so.

Source discrepancies

  • Beta end date discrepancy: the September 17 announcement said “through October 21”, while the official forum set and confirmed a closure on October 23. This wiki uses October 23.[7][9][3]
