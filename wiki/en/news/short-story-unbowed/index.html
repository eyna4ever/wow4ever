    Introduction

    Blizzard now has at least two official narrated short stories for WoW: Forever. “Unbowed”, published on October 26, follows Cormack, a former Scarlet Crusader raised as Forsaken. On November 13, “Cost of Honor”, told by the fireside by Demar Brant, turns to war, sacrifice and hard choices.[1][2]

    Key takeaways

    • October 26: official short story “Unbowed”, narrated and illustrated, about Cormack, who wonders whether an undead can still serve the Light.[1]
    • November 13: second official short story, “Cost of Honor”, told by the fireside by Demar Brant; it argues that a people’s strength also lies in the courage to protect one another.[2]

    “Unbowed” (October 26)

    Blizzard releases a narrated, illustrated short story about Cormack, a former Scarlet Crusader raised as Forsaken, who wonders whether an undead can still serve the Light.[1]

    “Cost of Honor” (November 13)

    On November 13, Blizzard releases a second short story, “Cost of Honor”, told by the fireside by Demar Brant; through tales of war, sacrifice and hard choices, it argues that a people’s strength also lies in the courage to protect one another.[2]
