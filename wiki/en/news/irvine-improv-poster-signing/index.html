In brief

On October 26, 2026 Blizzard announced a Forever poster signing on November 2, 2026 at the Irvine Improv (California). Free tickets open on TIXR on Tuesday, October 27 at 12:00 p.m. PDT. No French page was found on October 27 (HTTP 404).[1]

Key points

  • Event date: November 2, 2026, Irvine Improv.[1]
  • Free TIXR tickets from October 27 at 12:00 p.m. PDT.[1]
  • Entry only with a ticket / QR code for the reserved time slot.[1]
  • Game launch still announced for November 4, 2026.[1][2]

What the announcement says

Blizzard invites players to meet developers and fellow adventurers before the November 4 release. Attendance is limited. Guests should arrive early in their window; entry is by QR-code scan at the check-in tent.[1]

Irvine Improv is described as ADA compliant. Without a ticket, entry will not be possible.[1]
