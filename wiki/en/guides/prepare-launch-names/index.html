Getting ready for launch: two-part names and Early Name Reservation

How two-part names work, who can reserve from October 27 to November 3, regional start times and the rules to know.

In brief

In World of Warcraft: Forever each character has a two-part name that is unique within its region. Eligible purchasers can reserve up to three characters between October 27 and November 3, 2026, ahead of the November 4 launch.[1][2]

Key points

  • A first name and a “last name”: the complete combination must be unique within the region.[1]
  • Early Name Reservation: October 27 to November 3, up to three characters total across all rulesets.[2]
  • Requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack, Skyborne Epic Pack or Warcraft Forever Collection.[2][3]
  • First come, first served: no name is guaranteed.[2][1]

Two-part names

Each character has a first name and a secondary name. Two characters can share a first name as long as their full names differ; uniqueness applies across the region, not a realm.[1]

The secondary name can suggest a family, a place or a reputation; Blizzard’s example is Asha, Thalen and Mira Brightvale (Clairvallon in French). A guild can also adopt a shared theme.[1]

To hide your secondary name in the world: Escape > Options > Nameplates, then tick “My Secondary Name”; it still appears in chat.[1]

Naming rules

  • Names must follow the in-game code of conduct.[1]
  • Some words, spellings or repeated characters may be restricted, including names impersonating Blizzard.[1]
  • Both parts are evaluated together: splitting a restricted word across them will not necessarily work.[1]

During the reservation you can create up to three characters total across all rulesets: two on PvP leaves one for Normal or Roleplaying. The limit only applies to Early Name Reservation.[2]

The benefit only applies to the specific WoW account for which the World of Warcraft: Forever Digital Upgrade or Collector’s Edition was purchased.[2]

The official forum says character creation will open to everyone on October 27, so everyone can pick a ruleset and a name before launch.[4]

Getting ready, step by step

  • Update graphics drivers.[2]
  • Install or update the Battle.net desktop app.[2]
  • Select World of Warcraft, then the “World of Warcraft: Forever” game version above the Play button, and install.[2]
  • Click Play, choose a ruleset and create up to three characters.[2]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Reservation from October 27 to November 3, 2026 for eligible upgrades.[2][1]
  • Two-part names, unique per region.[1][5]

Still to be confirmed

  • Roleplay-PvP availability during the reservation is only “planned”.
  • The sources do not say how many characters per ruleset are allowed after launch.

Source discrepancies

  • The English text lists Europe in “CET”: on October 27 France is on winter time, so 18:00 in Paris.[2]
