    Introduction

    World of Warcraft: Forever can be played with a gamepad. Blizzard describes it as "available in Beta, with more improvements planned"; a keyboard is still needed for some actions, such as chat. The November 2 notes say "Gamepad Support has entered a functional Beta state".[2][1]

    Key takeaways

    • Movement, spells, interactions and UI navigation work with a gamepad.[1]
    • Targeting allies and enemies, looting and menus are supported.[2]
    • Settings are in Edit Mode and the Options menu.[1]
    • A keyboard is still needed for chat.[2]
    • All XInput-compatible controllers are supported, with Xbox and PlayStation button icons.[3]
    • To enable: Escape, Options, then Gamepad under Gameplay; three sets of four bars, 84 slots in total.[3]

    Official guide of November 3

    On November 3 Blizzard published an official gamepad guide, "Explore WoW: Forever with a Gamepad". It covers how to enable the gamepad and the basic controls.[3]

    • Compatibility: all XInput-compatible controllers, with Xbox and PlayStation button icons.[3]
    • To enable: press Escape to open the Game Menu, select Options, then Gamepad under Gameplay.[3]
    • Settings (Gamepad settings): Camera, HUD Modifier, Frame Focus Style, Action Bars, Targeting, Raid Targeting Options. On controllers with supported lighting, the light is red for a Horde character and blue for an Alliance character.[3]
    • Basic controls (Xbox names): left stick to move, right stick for the camera; LB friendly target, RB hostile target; X Interact, A Jump, B Back, Y Inspect (these buttons also have spell functions); Menu button: Main Menu; View button: Change Focus Frame.[3]
    • Camera zoom: hold LB + RB while moving the right stick forward and backward.[3]
    • Action bars: hold LT, RT or LT + RT to switch bars, release to return to the default bar. Three sets of four bars (28 slots per set), 84 slots in total; LB + RB + Left or Right on the D-pad switches sets. Official Mage example: Up = Polymorph (Sheep), LT + Up = Fireball, RT + Up = Frostbolt, LT + RT + Up = Blizzard (examples only, not fixed bindings).[3]
    • LB + RB shortcuts: + B Backpack, + Up Quest Tracker, + Down Chat, + Y Manage Buffs; also available from the main radial menu.[3]
    • Status: "functional beta"; a gamepad legend sits in the upper-left corner, but not every screen has gamepad footers yet. A keyboard is still needed to type in chat; a keyboard-free experience is the longer-term aim.[3]

    November 2 additions

    • Ground-targeted items can be placed from the gamepad action bar (press again to confirm).[1]
    • Ruleset selection screens, cinematic skipping, early exit from a flight (you land at the next flight point).[1]
    • Focus target: LB + RB + bottom button.[1]
    • Targeting friendly corpses and critters; enemy players can no longer be selected by friendly analog targeting.[1]
    • Pet movement command; reworked bag interface (free space, ammo, keyring).[1]
    • Better add-on compatibility.[1]

    WoW4ever AI editorial analysis

    Opinion · AIWoW4ever AI editorial analysis

    For gamepad players, the key point is the official status: the feature is in Beta, so settings and targeting may still change. The November 2 additions are mostly about comfort (corpses, pet, bags, flights). A keyboard is still useful for chat. This page will be updated when Blizzard publishes new gamepad notes. The November 3 guide confirms the beta status without presenting it as a flaw: the promise is about what comes next, with less keyboard over time. The 84 slots leave room to organise your abilities.

    Correction history

    • November 4, 2026: page created. A third-party site mentioned a "complete functional state"; the current official text says "functional Beta state".[1]
    • November 4, 2026: added the official guide of November 3 (enabling, basic controls, action bars, shortcuts, beta status).[3]

    What’s next?

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Gamepad support is announced as a Beta feature, with more improvements planned.[2]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Full official default button list: not published (the November 3 guide only covers basic controls).
