Choosing a ruleset: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Roleplay-PvP and Hardcore

World of Warcraft: Forever replaces realms with rulesets. This guide compares the five announced options and what is shared or kept apart.

In brief

World of Warcraft: Forever drops the realm-selection screen: you first pick a ruleset, which works like a broad player ecosystem. Five are announced: Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, Roleplay-PvP at launch, and Hardcore later.[1][2]

Key points

  • Normal, PvP and Roleplaying are available at launch; Roleplay-PvP was added on October 22 for launch.[1]
  • Hardcore arrives after launch, with no announced date.[1]
  • No grouping across rulesets or factions; battlegrounds may mix rulesets, except Hardcore.[1]
  • Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide, with Hardcore handled differently.[1][2]

Playing together

You can group, run dungeons and raid with players on the same ruleset. Cross-ruleset grouping is not supported; battlegrounds may include players from other rulesets, except Hardcore, which stays separate.[1]

Factions stay separate: Horde and Alliance cannot group together.[1][2]

This is not one giant realm: each ruleset is its own large ecosystem, designed so you keep crossing paths with familiar names.[2]

Moving between rulesets

You cannot freely hop between rulesets; to play elsewhere you create a new character. In Hardcore, a character that dies can transfer to another ruleset, including PvP.[1]

Collections and Legacy Points are account-wide. The exception: Hardcore progress can carry elsewhere, but progress from other rulesets does not flow back into Hardcore.[1][2]

PvP ruleset specifics

On the PvP ruleset an account can only create characters of one faction; Normal, Roleplaying and Hardcore allow both.[1]

To keep Horde and Alliance populations closer, Blizzard plans tools similar to Season of Discovery, where new character creation could be temporarily limited for a faction.[1]

Language preference

A language preference helps you meet players who share it: Escape > Options > System > Languages and Social > “Preferred Online Language”. It is account-wide with a 24-hour cooldown between changes.[1]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Normal, PvP and Roleplaying at launch; Hardcore after launch.[1]
  • Roleplay-PvP available at the November 4 launch.[1]
  • One faction per account on the PvP ruleset.[1]

Still to be confirmed

  • Roleplay-PvP for the October 27 name reservation is “planned” (“We also plan…”), not confirmed.
  • Whether the one-faction-per-account rule applies to Roleplay-PvP is not stated.
  • No date is given for Hardcore.

Source discrepancies

  • The French version of the article (September 21) does not yet mention Roleplay-PvP; only the English version updated October 22 does. No official French name was found: “JdR-JcJ” is this wiki’s wording.[1]
