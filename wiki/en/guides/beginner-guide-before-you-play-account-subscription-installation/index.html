    Introduction

    Before your first login, Blizzard’s official new player guide lists four steps: create a Battle.net account, subscribe or buy Game Time, install the game, and check your setup.[1]

    Key takeaways

    • World of Warcraft: Forever is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time.[1]
    • You play on Windows or Mac with keyboard and mouse; controller support is available in beta.[1]
    • In Europe, servers open on November 5, 2026 at 12:00 a.m. CET, which is November 4 at 23:00 UTC.[2]

    1. Create your Battle.net account

    Sign up from the Battle.net Login page, or open the Battle.net Shop and select Account > Create Account. Verify your email, choose a secure password and consider enabling two-factor authentication. If you already have an account, this wiki’s “Installing the game and recovering your account” page (link at the bottom) explains how to get back into it.[1]

    2. Subscribe or buy Game Time

    Forever needs no separate purchase: it is included with an active World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. Only the new Skyborne race requires the Heroic Pack or a higher digital upgrade.[1]

    3. Install and launch the game

    Download the Battle.net desktop app, sign in, install World of Warcraft and select World of Warcraft: Forever from the available game versions.[1]

    4. Check your setup

    The game runs on Windows and Mac with keyboard and mouse. Controller support is available in a beta state for players who prefer a gamepad. Before installing, review the current system requirements.[1]

    There is also a graphics card floor: Forever needs a GPU based on one of these microarchitectures or newer: AMD Graphics Core Next 1 (2012), NVIDIA Maxwell (2014) or Intel Skylake (2015). Blizzard ties it to the game’s new visual look and notes that most players already use compatible hardware.[3]

    • Compatible examples given by Blizzard: AMD Radeon HD 7850, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 9xx and 750/750 Ti, Intel HD Graphics 530; these cards are still well below the game’s minimum requirements.[3]
    • Unsupported examples: AMD Radeon HD 6950, NVIDIA GeForce GTX 760/770/780 and GTX 8xxM, Intel HD Graphics 6000.[3]
    • To check your card on Windows: Settings > System > Display > Advanced display, or Device Manager under Display adapters.[3]

    Optional editions

    Upgrade editions are optional and sold on the Battle.net Shop; for the game itself, only the Skyborne race depends on them.[4]

    • Skyborne Heroic Pack: the Skyborne race and their Zephras Isle starting quests, Early Name Reservation, the Cerulean Prideclaw mount (you buy the riding skill in game to use it), cosmetics, housing decor and one Invite-A-Friend Launch Code.[4]
    • Skyborne Epic Pack: everything in the Heroic Pack, plus 30 days of Game Time starting at launch, more cosmetics, pets, tabards and three Invite-A-Friend Launch Codes in total; it also gave access to the beta that opened on September 17.[4]
    • Warcraft Forever Collection (limited time): everything in the Epic Pack, plus Warcraft III: Reforged, the Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom campaign and figurine decor for Housing in modern World of Warcraft.[4]

    When can you log in?

    • Europe (CET): November 5, 2026, 12:00 a.m.[2]
    • UTC: November 4, 2026, 23:00.[2]
    • Americas (PST): November 4, 2026, 3:00 p.m.[2]

    What’s next?

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Forever is included with an active subscription or Game Time; the Skyborne require the Heroic Pack or higher.[1]
    • Launch: November 4, 2026 at 23:00 UTC, November 5 at 12:00 a.m. in Central Europe.[2]
    • Graphics card: AMD Graphics Core Next 1, NVIDIA Maxwell or Intel Skylake at minimum.[3]

    Still to be confirmed

    • The full requirements table (processor, memory, storage) is not published in any official article: Blizzard points to Forever’s Battle.net Shop page. Check it there before installing; we do not copy it so that we only cite dated Blizzard articles.
    • Edition prices are not given in any official article: the Battle.net Shop shows them for your region and currency.

    Source discrepancies

    • The English launch article’s title says November 4, the French title says November 5: it is the same moment (November 4, 23:00 UTC = November 5, 12:00 a.m. CET), per the article’s time table.[2]
    • For controllers, the English guide says “controller support” in beta, the French guide a “compatibility mode” in beta.[1]
