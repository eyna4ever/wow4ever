    Introduction

    The Skyborne, also known as the shen’dorei, or hidden people, are Forever’s new playable race. Their journey begins on Zephras Isle, a level 1–12 starting zone, and at character creation you choose whether they join the Horde or the Alliance.[3]

    Key takeaways

    • Two paths, one ancestry: the Windshapers (Horde) embrace elemental traditions, the High Order (Alliance) pursues the arcane legacy of their ancestors.[2]
    • Classes: Druid, Hunter, Rogue and Warrior for both; Shaman for Windshapers, Mage for the High Order.[4]
    • Access to the Skyborne and their starting zone requires the Heroic Pack or higher.[4]

    Origins: Skywall and Zephras Isle

    Thousands of years ago, a group of Highborne exiles forged a pact with elemental wind spirits and built a civilization within Skywall, one of the elemental planes. When you arrive on Zephras Isle, that civilization is in crisis: the air spirits that protected the island have vanished, ancient structures are failing, communities are becoming isolated and the people are divided over what comes next.[2]

    The Windshapers (Horde)

    The Windshapers (Horde) descend from the first shen’dorei high elves to receive the wind spirits’ gift of elemental skysight. A new generation leaves Skywall for Azeroth to learn what became of their elemental mentors and restore the wind spirits. They can become Shaman.[2]

    The High Order (Alliance)

    The High Order (Alliance) is made up of descendants of the Highborne scholars of Eldre’Thalas. When the spirits vanished, it realized that the Skyborne’s dependence on their elemental patrons left them vulnerable; its new generation returns to Azeroth to reclaim lost arcane knowledge so the shen’dorei can survive on their own. They can become Mages.[2]

    What’s next?

    Confirmed by official sources

    • The Skyborne are Forever’s only new race and can join either faction.[1]
    • Starting zone: Zephras Isle, levels 1–12.[2]

    Still to be confirmed

    • The Skyborne do not have a race page on the official World of Warcraft website yet (checked on November 3, 2026): everything told here comes from Blizzard’s Forever articles.
