    Introduction

    World of Warcraft: Forever is set after the events of Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core. At character creation, your race determines your appearance, starting location and racial abilities. Here is the history of the Alliance’s four original races, from Blizzard’s official sources only.[2][3]

    Key takeaways

    • The Alliance has four original races: Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes and Night Elves; High Order Skyborne can also join it (see the Skyborne page).[1]
    • Stormwind is described as the backbone of the Alliance; Dwarves and Gnomes come from Khaz Modan; the Night Elves’ capital, Darnassus, lies in the World Tree Teldrassil.[4][5][1][7]
    • Gnomeregan, the gnomes’ city, is lost at the time of Forever.[1]

    The Alliance

    According to Blizzard, the noble races of the Alliance are bound together by proud traditions of nobility, honor, faith, justice and sacrifice, and contribute their technical, arcane and spiritual wisdom toward a peaceful and just world.[1]

    Humans (Alliance)

    Humans are a young and highly versatile race, mastering combat, craftsmanship and magic with stunning efficiency. Their valor and optimism led them to build some of the world’s greatest kingdoms. In this troubled era, after generations of conflict, humanity seeks to rekindle its former glory.[1]

    Their capital is Stormwind. The human kingdom of Stormwind forms the backbone of the Alliance; its strength is its willingness to seek diplomatic solutions, and its citizens defend their home by the principles of honor and justice. The city’s monuments honor the sacrifices of past heroes.[4]

    Dwarves (Alliance)

    Dwarves are an ancient race descended from the earthen, titan-forged beings of living stone created when the world was young. Hailing from Ironforge in the snowy peaks of Khaz Modan, they are courageous defenders of the Alliance. Their capital, Ironforge, is carved into the mountain around a massive forge.[5]

    For ages, dwarves cared only for riches from the earth’s depths. Then records surfaced of a god-like race said to have given them life and an enchanted birthright. Since then they have pursued lost artifacts and ancient knowledge, and dwarven archaeologists are scattered throughout the globe.[1]

    Gnomes (Alliance)

    Though small in stature, the gnomes of Khaz Modan used their great intellect to secure a place in history. Their subterranean kingdom, Gnomeregan, was once a marvel of steam-driven technology, but the city was lost to a massive trogg invasion. Its builders are now vagabonds in the dwarven lands, aiding their allies as best they can.[1]

    The official race page highlights their optimism in the face of calamity, their boundless creativity and their commitment to the Alliance’s cause; Gnomeregan was their capital until that trogg invasion.[6]

    Night Elves (Alliance)

    Ten thousand years ago, the night elves founded a vast empire, but their reckless use of magic brought them to ruin. In grief, they withdrew to the forests and remained isolated until the return of their ancient enemy, the Burning Legion; with no other choice, they emerged to fight for their place in the new world.[1]

    Also known as the kaldorei, many still remember the War of the Ancients over ten thousand years ago, when they halted the Burning Legion’s first invasion of Azeroth. Their capital, Darnassus, lies in the boughs of Teldrassil, a massive World Tree, and reflects their reverence for nature.[7]

    What’s next?

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Forever is set between Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and Molten Core.[2]
    • Four original races playable in the Alliance, plus High Order Skyborne.[1]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Capitals and starting zones: Forever articles do not give them race by race (only Zephras Isle is announced, for the Skyborne). The capitals given here therefore come from the official race pages on the World of Warcraft website, which carry no publication date.
    • Leaders: only those named in Forever articles are mentioned, Sylvanas for the Forsaken and Thrall, the orcs’ young warchief. Today’s leaders and events from later expansions, found on the current race pages, are deliberately left out to stay in the game’s era.

    Source discrepancies

    • Gnomes: the current official page says the gnomes recovered sections of Gnomeregan; the Forever overview says the city is lost and its builders are vagabonds. We follow Forever.[1][6]
    • Night Elves: the current official page mentions an attack on Teldrassil during the Fourth War, an event from a later era; it is not included here.[7]
