PvP in World of Warcraft: Forever: honor, ranks and Darkspear Islands

Two distinct progressions (honor and rank points), seasons, and an announced 15-versus-15 battleground.

In brief

Forever PvP keeps the spirit of the original Honor System while simplifying it. Progression splits into honor (currency) and rank points (rank progression). A new battleground, Darkspear Islands, is announced as 15-versus-15.[1][2]

Key points

  • Honor comes from combat, finishing battlegrounds and PvP quests.[1]
  • Rank points advance rank and are separate from honor.[1]
  • Darkspear Islands: 15-versus-15, control points and flag captures.[2][3]
  • PvP updates are planned after launch, with no date.[2]

Honor

Honor is currency earned by taking part in PvP: combat, battlegrounds and related quests. Highlighted battlegrounds during their holiday weeks award increased Honor.[1]

Blizzard says Honor gains across battlegrounds were reviewed for more consistent rates, so players can enjoy the full map list.[1]

Rank points and seasons

Rank points advance a character’s rank. They are not the same as honor spent at vendors.[1]

The system is meant to give reasons to play throughout the season while staying familiar.[1]

Darkspear Islands

Darkspear Islands is announced as a 15-versus-15 battleground combining control points and flag captures. No detailed tactical map appears in the sources consulted.[2][3]

Link with rulesets

On the PvP ruleset an account only creates characters of one faction. Battlegrounds may mix players from other rulesets, except Hardcore.[4]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Honor = currency; rank points = rank progression.[1]
  • Darkspear Islands 15-versus-15 battleground announced.[2]

Still to be confirmed

  • The exact season calendar and full rank rewards are not fully published.
  • No detailed official Darkspear Islands map is in the sources consulted.
