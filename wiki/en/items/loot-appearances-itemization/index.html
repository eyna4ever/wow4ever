    Items: dungeon loot, appearances and itemization in Forever

    • In brief
    • Key points
    • Reworked stats
    • Dungeon and world loot
    • Appearance collection
    • Confirmed / to be confirmed
    • Sources

    In brief

    Forever keeps Classic’s stats but reworks several so gear varies by role and activity. Every dungeon drop has been re-examined, unique boss items move to blue quality and appearance collection follows rules designed for group play.[1]

    Key points

    • Unique dungeon boss items: blue quality; improved set bonuses.[1]
    • Bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare dungeon drop: appearance for every eligible looter.[1]
    • Epic raid drop: appearance only for the player it binds to.[1]

    Reworked stats

    • Spell, melee and ranged hit chance are combined, as are critical strike chances.[1]
    • Weapon skill works as before, but each item grants less of it.[1]
    • Bonus healing also includes one third as much bonus damage.[1]
    • New effects tied to environments (woodlands, mountains, deserts) or creature types, such as X’caliboar.[1]
    • More situational trinkets, such as the Worgenbane Talisman.[1]

    Dungeon and world loot

    Every dungeon drop has been re-examined: memorable items stay memorable, less exciting ones are adjusted and hundreds of new drops are added. World-drop epics are improved and hundreds of new rare-creature drops appear.[1]

    Advanced camp feature blueprints drop from specific dungeon bosses.[2]

    Appearance collection

    Appearance collection rules (Blizzard, 2026-09-13)[1]
    CaseRule
    Bind-on-pickup Uncommon or Rare item, in a dungeonAppearance granted to all eligible looters when the item is looted
    Epic raid dropAppearance granted only to the player the item binds to
    Poor or Common quality itemAppearance granted as soon as it is looted, without binding
    Item typePlate to plate, cloth to cloth
    LevelHigh-level appearances usable from level 60

    Transmog can be disabled at a transmogrifier; it is off automatically in Classic Mode, and collecting continues in the background. Cosmetic-only items will be rare.[1]

    What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

    Confirmed by official sources

    • Unique dungeon boss items in blue quality; improved set bonuses.[1]
    • Appearance rules: shared in dungeons, reserved for epic raid drops, Poor/Common on loot.[1]
    • Advanced camp blueprints from specific dungeon bosses.[2]

    Still to be confirmed

    • Per-boss loot tables, detailed item stats and drop rates: not published by Blizzard; no third-party database reused.
    • List of new rare-creature drops and world-drop epics: not published.
    • Build number: not given in the official sources consulted.
