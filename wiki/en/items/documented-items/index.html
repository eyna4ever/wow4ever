In brief

Forever does not yet have a full public item database. This wiki only lists items named in official articles: highlighted quest rewards, camp objects, and subscription-offer bonuses tied to Forever.[1][2][3]

Key points

  • Ladimore Heirloom Ring: announced caster reward (Mor’ladim quest, Duskwood).[1]
  • Worgenbane Talisman: item named in the items batch sources.[1]
  • Camp objects (sharpening wheel, banner, incense…): see the camping article.[2]
  • 12-month subscription offer: tabard, backpack and Azurky pet for Forever once the game launches.[3]

Highlighted quest rewards

The Ladimore Heirloom Ring is cited as a caster reward on the Mor’ladim-related quest in Duskwood. The Worgenbane Talisman appears in the same documentary set.[1]

Subscription bonuses tied to Forever

The special 6- or 12-month subscription offer includes bonuses usable in Forever after the November launch: Tabard of Eternal Exploration, Eternal Explorer’s Backpack and the Azurky pet.[3]

Bonuses arrive by October 31, 2026 and must be redeemed by April 30, 2027. Forever access itself remains included with a subscription or Game Time from November 4.[3][4]

What this wiki does not do

No dungeon or raid loot table is invented. When Blizzard publishes more, new draft notices can be added.[5]

What is confirmed / what remains to be confirmed

Confirmed by official sources

  • Ladimore ring and Worgenbane talisman named in deep-dive / batch sources.[1]
  • Forever cosmetic bonuses in the 12-month subscription offer.[3]

Still to be confirmed

  • Exact item stats: not copied when absent from the consulted official text.
  • Loot tables for the December 9 raids: not published in the raid overview.

Source discrepancies
